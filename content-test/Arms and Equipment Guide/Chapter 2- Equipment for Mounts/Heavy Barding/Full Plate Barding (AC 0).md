**Full Plate Barding (AC 0)**

Full plate barding, like [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Full Plate Armor (AC 1)|full plate armor]], is the highest achievement of the best armorers. It is available only in late period campaigns and will be very rare at the best of times; only the most advanced and richest kingdoms can master the techniques of its construction. The services of a master armorer are required. Full plate barding weighs 90 pounds. 

**Half Full Plate:** This gives the equivalent of full plate protection to the front of the horse only. Again, this barding, if encountered, is almost certain to be ceremonial armor. It weighs 70 pounds.
