**Ring Mail**

This form of [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Chain Mail (AC 5)|chain mail]] is made by sewing large metal rings to a leather or cloth backing. Ring mail has the same role in early-period campaigns that chain mail has in later ones. In later campaigns, it is more expensive to buy than chain mail, weighs a comparable amount, provides worse protection (AC 7), and suffers all the maintenance problems of [[Padded Armor (AC 8)#81190c70|padded]] and [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Studded Leather Armor (AC 7)|studded leather armor]]. Few human groups, other than town militias and bandit gangs, use ring mail to any significant degree.
