**Inflict Pain**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Telepathy **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con â€“4 **Initial Cost:** contact **Maintenance Cost:** 2/round **Range:** Touch **Preparation Time:** 1 **Area of Effect:** Individual **Prerequisites:** [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Sciences/Mindlink|Mindlink]], [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Contact|Contact]]This is a particularly nasty form of torture. It causes no actual harm to the subject and leaves no marks or scars, but causes excruciating pain of any sort the telepath desires. Only evil characters can learn this power freely; others find their alignments twisting toward evil if they wish to inflict pain. If the victim is an NPC who is being questioned, he must make a saving throw vs. paralyzation to withstand this agony.**

If the psionicist finds a way to use this power in combat, the victim must also make a saving throw vs., paralyzation. Success means he grits his teeth and keeps on fighting. Failure means the pain imposes a four-point penalty on the character's attack rolls that round, or disrupts and ruins a spell.

*Power Score* - The pain is so excruciating that the victim passes out for 1d10 rounds.

*20* - This particular contact is broken.
