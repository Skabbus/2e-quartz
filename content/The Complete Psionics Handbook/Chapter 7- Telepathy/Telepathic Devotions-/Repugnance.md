That character is overwhelmed with loathing for the thing, and he will seek to destroy it as completely and quickly as possible.**

If this destruction is strongly against the character's alignment (such as making a temple repugnant to its cleric), the character gets to make a saving throw vs. spells to shake off the effect.

*Power Score* - No save is allowed.

*20* - The victim is aware of the attempt.
