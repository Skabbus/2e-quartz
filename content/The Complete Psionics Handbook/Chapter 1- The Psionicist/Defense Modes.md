## Defense Modes

Psionic *defense modes* are special powers which all psionicists acquire naturally in time. All defense modes belong to the telepathic discipline. Psionicists learn these powers automatically as they gain new experience levels regardless of whether or not they have access to the telepathic discipline. Defense modes do not count toward the psionicist's maximum number of powers as listed in Table 4. Nor are they counted when characters determine the relative number of sciences and devotions they can acquire within a given discipline.

There are five psionic defense modes:

* [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Mind Blank|Mind Blank]]
* [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Thought Shield|Thought Shield]]
* [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Mental Barrier|Mental Barrier]]
* [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Sciences/Tower of Iron Will|Tower of Iron Will]]
* [[The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Intellect Fortress|Intellect Fortress]]

Each is described in [Chapter 7, "Telepathy."](Ch_7.htm) All psionicists automatically know one of these powers at 1st level (player's choice). They learn another defense mode of the player's choice every other level - at 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th.
