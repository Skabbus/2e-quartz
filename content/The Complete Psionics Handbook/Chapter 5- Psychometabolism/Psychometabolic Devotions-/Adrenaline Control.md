**Adrenaline Control**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con -3 **Initial Cost:** 8 **Maintenance Cost:** 4/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** Personal **Prerequisites:** noneBy controlling the production and release of adrenalin in his system, the psionicist can give himself temporary physical boosts on demand. When he increases his adrenalin, the character gains 1d6 points, which he can add directly to his Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution scores however he chooses. He enjoys all the normal bonuses for high physical attributes while this power is in effect.**

If the psionicist's power check result is a 1, he overtaxes his system with this adrenalin. He still gets the boost, but he loses twice that many hit points, too.
