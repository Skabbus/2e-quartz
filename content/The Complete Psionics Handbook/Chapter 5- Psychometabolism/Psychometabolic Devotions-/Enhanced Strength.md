**Enhanced Strength**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Wis -3 **Initial Cost:** varies **Maintenance Cost:** varies **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneA psionicist can increase his physical Strength score to a maximum of 18 through this devotion. The PSP cost is twice the number of points he adds to his Strength score. (If he fails, he loses half this amount; see Chapter 1.) The maintenance cost per round equals the number of Strength points he has added.**

Physical Strength cannot be raised above 18 psionically. The psionicist does not qualify for exceptional Strength bonuses if he raises his Strength to 18.

*Power Score* - The psionicist can raise his strength to 18/00, with each 25% increase costing an additional PSP.*20* - The power backfires and lowers Strength by 1d6 until arrested by this power.
