**Graft Weapon**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con -5 **Initial Cost:** 10 **Maintenance Cost:** 1/round **Range:** touch **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneWith this power the psionicist can graft any one-handed melee weapon directly onto his body. He picks up the weapon, activates this power, and the weapon becomes an extension of the character's arm. Assuming the character is proficient with that type of weapon, he gains a +l bonus to attack rolls and damage. If he is not proficient with the weapon, he suffers the usual nonproficiency penalties, but still gets the +l bonus.**

*Power Score* - The +1 bonus for attacks and damage increases to +4.

*20* - The weapon has been weakened; it will break on any attack roll of 1.
