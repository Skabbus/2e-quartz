**Displacement**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con -3 **Initial Cost:** 6 **Maintenance Cost:** 3/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneThis power resembles the displacer beast's natural ability to make itself appear to be up to 3 feet from his actual location. The psionicist decides where this false image will appear. This is a very effective means of protecting oneself from attack, giving the psionicist a two-point bonus on his armor class (lowering it two points). *True seeing* will reveal the character's real location.**

*Power Score* - The AC bonus is +4*20* - No effect.
