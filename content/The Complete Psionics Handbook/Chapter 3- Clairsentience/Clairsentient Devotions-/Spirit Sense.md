**Spirit Sense**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Clairsentience **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Wis -3 **Initial Cost:** 10 **Maintenance Cost:** na **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** 15-yard radius **Prerequisites:** noneUsing this power allows the psionicist to sense the presence of "spirits" within 15 yards - meaning ghosts, banshees, wraiths, haunts, heucuvas, and revenants. If a spirit frequently haunts the location at hand, the psionicist will know it. He will also know when a spirit is within 15 yards, but he won't be able to pinpoint its location.**

*Power Score* - The psionicist knows the exact location of the spirits he senses.

*20* - The psionicist has aggravated the spirits (DM determines exact result).
