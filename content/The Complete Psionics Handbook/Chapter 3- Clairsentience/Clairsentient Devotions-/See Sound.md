**See Sound**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Clairsentience **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Wis -3 **Initial Cost:** 6 **Maintenance Cost:** 3/round **Range:** Special **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** Personal **Prerequisites:** noneThis power enables a character to perceive sound waves visually - with his eyes - by converting the sound waves to light impulses (it works in much the same way as feel light). Only a character who can see normally can use this power. The psionicist can see sound even in darkness, because sound waves do not require light. The character can still be "blinded" by silence, however.**

*Power Score* - The psionicist can maintain this power for 1 PSP per round.

*20* - Loud sounds cause "blindness", and all other sounds are as disturbing as bright lights.
