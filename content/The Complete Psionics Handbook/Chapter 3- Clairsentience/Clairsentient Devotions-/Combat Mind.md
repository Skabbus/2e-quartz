**Combat Mind**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Clairsentience **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Int -4 **Initial Cost:** 5 **Maintenance Cost:** 4/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** Personal **Prerequisites:** NoneA character using this power has an unusually keen understanding of his enemies and their fighting tactics. As a result, the psionicist's side in combat gains a -1 bonus when making initiative die rolls. This is cumulative with any other modifiers which may apply.**

*Power Score* - The psionicist (but not his companions) also gains a +1 AC bonus.

*20* - The psionicist and his companions suffer a +1 initiative penalty.
