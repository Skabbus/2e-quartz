## Clothesmaking, Crude

(Proficiency)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Clothesmaking, Crude |
| Crossover Group | General |
| Relevant Ability | Intelligence |
| Check Modifier | -1 |
| # of Slots | 1 Slot |

This proficiency enables a character to create simple garments from furs, skins, leaves, and other natural materials. Although crude clothing isn't attractive or stylish, it's generally comfortable and functional. Fur cloaks, grass skirts, and hide loincloths are typical examples. ([[The Complete Barbarian's Handbook/Chapter 5- Weapons and Equipment/Clothing and Body Decoration|See Chapter 5 for more about primitive clothing]].)

**Crossover Group:** General.
