## Weaponsmithing, Crude (Proficiency)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Weaponsmithing, Crude |
| Crossover Group | Warrior |
| Relevant Ability | Wisdom |
| Check Modifier | -3 |
| # of Slots | 1 Slot |

This proficiency allows the character to make simple nonmetallic weapons using stone, wood, bone, and other natural substances. The character can only fashion weapons for which he has weapon proficiencies. For example, a character with a proficiency in spears can fashion crude spears but not crude axes.

Table 36 summarizes the construction times for various weapons. The DM may augment this list with additional weapons (see Chapter 5 for ideas).

After completing a weapon, the character must make a proficiency check. If he has the hunting proficiency, he modifies his checks by +3. If the check fails, the weapon is so badly flawed that it can't be used. On a roll of 20, it breaks on the first use. On a roll of 1, the weapon won't break unless struck against a harder material.

**Barbarians:** A barbarian may only fashion weapons commonly used in his homeland.

**Crossover Group:** General ([[The Complete Barbarian's Handbook/Chapter 4- Proficiencies/Proficiencies#Table_30_Nonweapon_Proficiencies_(Fighters|Table 30]]) lists Crossover Group as Warrior, not General.)
