## Other Kits

Other kits dealing with barbarians have appeared previously — there have even been a few kits for wizards from barbaric tribes! Previous treatments of the barbarian theme include:

* *The Complete Fighter:* [[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Barbarian|Barbarian]], [[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Role-Playing/The One-Warrior-Type Campaign/Beast-Riders|Beast Rider]], [[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Berserker|Berserker]], [[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Savage|Savage]], [[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Wilderness Warrior|Wilderness Warrior]].
* *The Complete Priest:* [[The Complete Priest's Handbook/Priest Kits/The Priest Kits/Barbarian-Berserker Priest|Barbarian/Berserker Priest]], [[The Complete Priest's Handbook/Priest Kits/The Priest Kits/Savage Priest|Savage Priest]].
* *The Complete Wizard:* [[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Chapter 3- Wizard Kits/The Wizard Kits/The Kits/Anagakok|Anagakok]], [[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Chapter 3- Wizard Kits/The Wizard Kits/The Kits/Savage Wizard|Savage Wizard]].
* *The Complete Bard:* [[The Complete Bard's Handbook/Chapter 2- Bard Kits/List of Kits/Skald|Skald]].
* *The Complete Ranger:* [[The Complete Ranger's Handbook/Chapter 4- Ranger Kits/List of Kits/Feralan|Feralan]].

If any of these are considered for use, the DM should decide how and if they fit into the campaign setting.
