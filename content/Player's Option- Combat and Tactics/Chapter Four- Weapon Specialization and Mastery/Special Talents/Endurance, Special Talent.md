**Endurance\*** (2 slots/4 CP) Con/FitnessGroup: Warrior Initial rating: 3This proficiency allows a character to perform strenuous physical activity twice as long as a normal character before fatigue and exhaustion set in. If the fatigue rules from [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter One- Player's Option Combat System/Ending the Combat Round/Fatigue/Fatigue|Chapter One]] are in play, a character with this proficiency increases his fatigue points by 50%.[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
