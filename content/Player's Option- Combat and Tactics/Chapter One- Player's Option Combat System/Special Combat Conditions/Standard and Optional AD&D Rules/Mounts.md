**Mounts**

Mounts provide their rider with a +1 bonus to attack rolls when fighting against unmounted opponents, while the opponents suffer a –1 penalty to attacks against the rider (there is no penalty to attack the mount itself). Most mounts are fairly large and can overrun smaller creatures. A normal human on horseback can use his mount’s speed and size to trample his opponents underfoot (see [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter One- Player's Option Combat System/Combat Actions/Overruns|Overrun]]). Many mounts are also capable of making attacks along with their rider.
