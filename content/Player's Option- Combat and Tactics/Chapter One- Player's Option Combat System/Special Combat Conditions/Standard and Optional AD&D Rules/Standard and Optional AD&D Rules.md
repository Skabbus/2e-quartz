Standard and Optional AD&D Rules

These rules can all be found in the *Player’s Handbook* and/or the *Dungeon Master Guide*. Even if they are listed as optional rules in the core rule books, they are a standard part of the *Player’s Option* combat system. They are presented again here (with any necessary combat system alterations and additions) for ease of reference. Refer to Chapter 9: Combat in the [PHB](..\PHB\DD01661.htm)and [DMG](..\DMG\DD00383.htm)for more details.
