**Half-Move Actions**

Half-move actions allow a character to move up to half his normal movement rate and still perform some other action, such as attacking with a weapon or firing missiles. There are, however, limits to what can be done or how far a character can move and still accomplish these actions. Half-move actions include:· Attack· Charge· Fire/Throw Missiles (half the normal ROF)· Guard· Unarmed Combat· Withdraw[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
