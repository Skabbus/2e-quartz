Ending the Combat Round

After all the combat actions have been resolved, it is time to finish up the round and get ready for the next one. The end of the round is a time to take care of any loose ends or bookkeeping that needs to be looked after. The four major items that are addressed at the end of the round are *retreats, fatigue,* *morale,* and *status.*[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
