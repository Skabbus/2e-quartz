**Formal Morale Checks**

A formal morale check is a comparison of a 2d10 roll against the creature’s base Morale rating, modified for the situation. An extensive list of modifiers appear on the DM Screen and under Morale in Chapter 9: Combat in the [Dungeon Master Guide](..\DMG\DD00476.htm).
