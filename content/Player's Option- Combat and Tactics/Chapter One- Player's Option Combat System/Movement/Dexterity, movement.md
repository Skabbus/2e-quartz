**Dexterity**Add the character’s reaction adjustment to his base movement rate. If you are also playing with the [Skills & Powers](..\SP\DD02869.htm#18d645e0) rules, use the adjustment for Dexterity/Balance instead.

*For example, Loftos the Swift, a human fighter, has a Strength of 17 and a Dexterity of 16. His Strength gives him a hit probability bonus of +1, and his Dexterity gives him a reaction bonus of +1, too. His base movement rate, therefore, is 12+1+1, or 14.*[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
