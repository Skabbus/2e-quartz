Limited Visibility

Fighting at night or in dark settings is difficult—especially if your foe can see better than you can. As noted in the [DMG](..\DMG\DD00622.htm), characters fighting in darkness or heavy fog suffer penalties to their attack rolls, damage bonuses, and Armor Classes.
