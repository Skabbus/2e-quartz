**Starlight or Dense Fog or Heavy Rain**

Characters suffer a –3 to attack rolls and saving throws, a –2 penalty to AC, and only get ½ their normal damage bonus for exceptional Strength. Nothing can be seen more than 50 yards away (10 yards for dense fog or cloudy moonless nights), so spell and missile ranges are very limited.
