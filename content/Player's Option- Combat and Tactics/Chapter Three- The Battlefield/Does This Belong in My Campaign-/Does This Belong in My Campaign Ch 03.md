Does This Belong in My Campaign? Even if you are not using the *Player’s Option* combat system presented in [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter One- Player's Option Combat System/Player's Option Combat System|Chapter One]], you’ll still find that most of the material in this chapter can be useful in any AD&D campaign. The information on movement, cover, and encounter ranges can go a long way toward making your battles more visual and exciting. Your players will also enjoy the new tactical opportunities (and problems) this chapter presents.
