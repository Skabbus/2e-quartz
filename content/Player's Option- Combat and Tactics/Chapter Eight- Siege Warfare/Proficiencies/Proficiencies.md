Proficiencies

The following proficiencies are applicable to warfare and the operation of war equipment. They are acquired the same way standard [PHB](..\PHB\DD01537.htm) proficiencies are.

**Artillerist** (1 Slot) Charisma/LeadershipGroup: WarriorA character with this proficiency can direct the siting and operation of a bombardment engine. The maximum number of engines the character can control is equal to 1/3 of the character’s Charisma/Leadership score, provided that the engines are no farther apart than the character can sprint in a single round.

**Vehicle Handling** (1 Slot) Dexterity/DodgeGroup: WarriorThis proficiency allows the character to control a wagon or chariot under difficult circumstances. The character can roll against this proficiency when a driving check is normally required.
