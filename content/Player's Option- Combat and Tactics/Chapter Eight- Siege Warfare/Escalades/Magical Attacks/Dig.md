***Dig***

This spell allows the caster to dig through earth ramparts at the rate given in the spell description. If a hole dug through a rampart collapses, the area above it becomes damaged (see the [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Eight- Siege Warfare/Sieges/Sieges|Sieges section]], below).
