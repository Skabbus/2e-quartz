***Wall of Fire***

This spell can set fire to wooden structures as mentioned in War Machine vs. War Machine, above, if it is placed against them. Roll for a fire once for each level of the caster. If a wooden construction passes through a *wall of fire*, it suffers 1d3 points of structural damage plus one point per caster level and can start a fire as noted above.
