Does This Belong in My Campaign? While the rest of this book assumes that you’ll be playing with the full [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Six- Critical Hits/Critical Hits Tables/Bludgeoning vs. Humanoids, critical hit table#bcdf214c|critical hit tables]] and rules, you’ll find that this chapter can be completely disregarded with no ill effects. More than anything else in this book, critical hits are optional; if the DM and players don’t want them, the AD&D game works fine without them.

However, there is one thing worth mentioning: if monsters have to suffer critical hits, so do player characters. Otherwise, game balance quickly goes out the window.
