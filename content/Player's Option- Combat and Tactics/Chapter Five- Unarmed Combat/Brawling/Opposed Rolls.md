Opposed Rolls

The brawling procedures make extensive use of opposed rolls. See [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Two- Combat Options/Attack Options/The Opposed Roll|Chapter Two]] for a discussion of how to use opposed rolls in combat. Note that in an opposed ability check a die roll of 20 is always a failure, even if the effective score is 20 or more. In an opposed attack roll, a die roll of 1 is always a failure, even if adjustments to the roll would allow for success.
