Does This Belong in My Campaign? Battle Tactics relies heavily on the *Player’s Option* combat system, presented in Chapter One. Although the basic concepts are still sound, you won’t see much game benefit to these strategies unless you are resolving battles with the combat system.

The other four parts of this chapter—[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Two- Combat Options/Attack Options/Attack Options|Attack Options]], [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Two- Combat Options/Fighting Styles/Fighting Styles|Fighting Styles]], [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Two- Combat Options/Dueling/Dueling|Dueling]], and [[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Five- Unarmed Combat/Brawling/Brawling|Brawling]]—can all be incorporated into existing campaigns together or independently. They do not require the combat system to be used in your campaign. The information in this chapter replaces the related topics in *The Complete Fighter’s Handbook*.[[Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
