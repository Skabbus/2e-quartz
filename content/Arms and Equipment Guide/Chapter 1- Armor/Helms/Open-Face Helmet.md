**Open-Face Helmet**

This class of open-face helmet, made of reinforced leather or metal, covers most of the head, save the face and neck. These helmets commonly provide protection for the nose (this piece of the helm is called a *nasal*).

Open-face helmets are popular with officers and leaders, and are appropriately worn with medium-weight armors like [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Brigandine Armor (AC 6)|brigandine]] and [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Chain Mail (AC 5)|chain mail]]. **Vision and Hearing:** -1 penalty to Vision checks; -2 penalty to Hearing checks (such helms cover the ears entirely except for a small hole).
