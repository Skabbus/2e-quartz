**Great Helm**

A great helm is any massive metal helm that covers the entire head, including the neck and often the upper shoulders. The only gaps in the protection are found in the narrow slits that allow limited vision, and some small holes to permit breathing. The visor is not normally removable.

It is rare to find a great helm used with armor lighter than [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Banded Mail (AC 4)|banded mail]]. Great helms are most often found in combination with [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Plate Mail (AC 3)|plate mail]] or heavier armor. 

**Vision and Hearing:** -3 penalty to Vision checks; -4 penalty to Hearing checks.(See also [Armor](..\PHB\DD01631.htm), Player’s Handbook and [Magical Armor and Shields](..\DMG\DD01071.htm), Dungeon Master Guide)[[Arms and Equipment Guide/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
