**Coif**

A coif is a padded chain mail hood. It fits snugly around the neck and over the head, except the face. It usually comes with or is built into a suit of [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Chain Mail (AC 5)|chain mail]] or mail-based armor. Often, a heavily armored knight will wear a chain mail coif under a [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Helms/Great Helm|great helm]]. This allows him to remove his great helm for a better view of the field while maintaining a fair level of head protection. 

**Vision and Hearing:** Identical Vision and Hearing check penalties as those associated with a cap (above). If a great helm is worn, penalties are assigned for the great helm and are not cumulative with those of the coif.
