**Clawed Gloves and Shoes**

Similar to crampons, claws give climbers and mountaineers greater ability to cling to handholds and ledges. Both types are held on with leather straps and add a +5% to Climbing and a +1 to Mountaineering.

Cost: Gloves 3 gp, Shoes 5 gp[[Arms and Equipment Guide/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
