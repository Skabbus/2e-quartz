The *light warhorse* category includes ponies and riding horses (the latter reflects custom and use rather than the ability of the horse to carry actual weight).

*Limits:* A horse can wear half barding one class heavier than its maximum full barding. Flying creatures are limited to [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Light Barding/Leather Barding (AC 6)|leather]] and [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Light Barding/Padded Barding (AC6)#55ad43fc|padded barding]] only.*[[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Light Barding/Padded Barding (AC6)#55ad43fc|Padded]]* includes quilted armor.*[[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Medium Barding/Scale Barding (AC 5)#2fe80346|Scale]]* includes studded leather, [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Medium Barding/Brigandine Barding (AC 5)|brigandine]], and [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 1- Armor/Armor/Ring Mail|ring mail]] (weights will differ).*[[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Heavy Barding/Banded Barding (AC 3)#94383722|Banded]]* includes [[Arms and Equipment Guide/Chapter 2- Equipment for Mounts/Heavy Barding/Splint Barding (AC 3)|splint]].
