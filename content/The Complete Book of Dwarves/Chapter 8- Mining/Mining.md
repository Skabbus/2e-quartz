**Chapter 8: Mining**Mining**New dwarf strongholds are established for a wide variety of reasons, but they are almost always sited near substantial deposits of minerals or gems. This chapter contains rules for establishing and operating mines. It is assumed that most existing strongholds are built around profitable and extensive ones.**
