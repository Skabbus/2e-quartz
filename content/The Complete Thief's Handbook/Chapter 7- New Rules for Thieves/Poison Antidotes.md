**Poison Antidotes**

(Optional Rule)**

**In AD&D® 2nd Edition rules, a character with healing proficiency is able to aid poisoned individuals. This is described in [Chapter Five](..\PHB\DD01570.htm) of the *Player's Handbook*. We reproduce those rules here with additional options, including ones for those campaigns that are not using nonweapon proficiency rules.**
