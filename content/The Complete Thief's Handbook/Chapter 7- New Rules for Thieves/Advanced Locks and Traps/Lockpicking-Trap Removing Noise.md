**Lockpicking/Trap Removing Noise**A thief attempting to open a lock or remove a trap with complete stealth can try to do so. He suffers a -10% chance to his ability rating, but will perform the task quietly on any roll except 01-10%. In the latter case the lock or trap makes a sharp click, snap, or other sound, audible to anyone within earshot.

If a thief fails a silent attempt, he cannot try to open the same lock normally.
