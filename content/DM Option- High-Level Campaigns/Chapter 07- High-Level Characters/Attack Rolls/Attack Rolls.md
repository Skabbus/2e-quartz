Attack Rolls

Like a saving throw, there is always a chance that a melee or missile attack can fail. No matter what the attacker’s THAC0, an attack roll of 1 is always a miss. In addition, a character’s THAC0 advancement is limited—professional skills and training only extend a creature’s natural talents so far. Warriors, naturally, can improve THAC0 the most. [[DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Tables/Table 39- THAC0 Limits|Table 39]] shows THAC0 limits for all four character groups. Monsters have no THAC0 limits.
