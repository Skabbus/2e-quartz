**Table 32:**

Durations

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration** | **Difficulty** |
| Instantaneous1 | 0 |
| 1 round | 1 |
| 1 turn | 3 |
| 1 hour | 5 |
| 6 hours | 7 |
| 12 hours | 10 |
| 1 day | 15 |
| 1 week | 30 |
| 1 month | 50 |
| 1 year | 70 |
| Permanent2 | 100 |

1 Spells with instantaneous durations have permanent effects. For example, a strike occurs in an instant, but the damage it inflicts remains until healed. Spells with longer durations cease to affect their targets once their durations expire.

2 Making a spell permanent usually drains a point of Constitution from the caster, see the individual spell descriptions for exceptions.
