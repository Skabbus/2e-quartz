Don’t Inflate-Enhance[[DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Chapter 01- The Seven Maxims/Intelligent Adversaries/Intelligent Adversaries|Chapter One]] points out the detrimental effects power inflation has on a campaign. Resist the temptation to create bizarre new creatures with mind-boggling power, to introduce legions of high-level villains, or to place high-level player characters in direct conflict with deities.

Enhancement means finding ways to challenge PCs without overpowering them. [[DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Chapter 01- The Seven Maxims/Intelligent Adversaries/Minimizing Personal Risks|Chapter One]]’s discussion of maximizing strengths and minimizing weaknesses describes one type of enhancement.
