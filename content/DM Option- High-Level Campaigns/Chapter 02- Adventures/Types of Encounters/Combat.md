**Combat**

:** The encounter is intended to deplete the party’s resources, particularly hit points and spells, through some form of attack. Combats can be pretty dull for high-level characters unless the opposition follows a logical tactical plan that allows them to challenge the party and create the illusion of danger. See the [[DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Chapter 02- Adventures/Planning Combats/Planning Combats|Planning Combats]] section for some tips.
