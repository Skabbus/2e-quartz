**Alignment***

The Complete Fighter's Handbook* follows all normal game rules for character alignments. Once the player has chosen an alignment for his character, he needs to have his choice approved by the DM; it may be that his choice will clash excessively with the alignments of other characters in the party, so the DM is within his rights to disapprove any alignment choice. (The chaotic evil fighter who wants to play with the troupe of wandering paladins *will* be a problem.)[[The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
