**Intelligence and Proficiencies**

We're going to be showing you a lot of interesting things you can do with the Weapon Proficiencies rules. Therefore, you need to use the rule for extra Proficiencies given on page 51 of the *Player's Handbook*. There, it says that you may, with the DM's permission, take extra proficiencies when first created equal to the number of extra languages the character gets from high Intelligence (see [Table 4](..\PHB\DD01432.htm), page 19, *Player's Handbook*). These extra proficiencies may be divided as the player chooses between Weapon Proficiencies and Nonweapon Proficiencies.
