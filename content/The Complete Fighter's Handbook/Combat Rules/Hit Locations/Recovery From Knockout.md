**Recovery From Knockout**

A Knockout result can be reached from Punching and Martial Arts attacks and from the Sap maneuver.

When a character is knocked out, he must immediately make a System Shock percentile roll (see "[Table 3: Constitution](..\PHB\DD01430.htm)," in the *Player's Handbook*. Page 15.) If he makes the roll, he will awaken in 2d6 minutes. If he fails the roll, he will awaken in 2d6 hours.
