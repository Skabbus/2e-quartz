**Protection from Gas**

:** (Reading time—3) This scroll generates a 10-foot diameter sphere of protection centered on the reader. All within the area are immune to the effects of any gas—poison gas, gaseous breath weapons, spells that generate gas (such as *stinking cloud* and *cloudkill*), and all similar forms of noxious, toxic vapors. The protection lasts for 1d4+4 rounds.
