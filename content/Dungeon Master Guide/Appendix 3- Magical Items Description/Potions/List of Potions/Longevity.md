**Longevity**

:** The *longevity* potion reduces the character's age by 1d12 years, restoring youth and vigor. The entire potion must be consumed to achieve the desired result. It is also useful as a counter to magical or monster-based aging attacks.

Each time one drinks a *longevity* potion, there is a 1% cumulative chance the effect will be the reverse of what the consumer wants—all age removed by previous drinks will be restored![[Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
