**Philter of Love**

:** This potion causes the individual drinking it to become charmed (see [charm](..\PHB\DD02149.htm) spells) with the first creature seen after consuming the draught. The imbiber may actually become enamored if the creature is of similar race and of the opposite sex. Charm effects wear off in 1d4+4 turns, but the enamoring effects last until a *dispel magic* spell is cast upon the individual.
