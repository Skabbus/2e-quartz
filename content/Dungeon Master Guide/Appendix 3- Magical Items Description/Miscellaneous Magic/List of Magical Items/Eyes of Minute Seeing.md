**Eyes of Minute Seeing**

:** In appearance, *eyes of minute seeing* are much like other magical lenses, but they enable the wearer to see 100 times better at distances of 1 foot or less. Thus, tiny seams, minute marks, even the impression left from writing can be seen. Secret compartments and hidden joints can be noted and the information acted upon. The effect of wearing just one of these crystals is the same as that given for *eyes of the eagle.*[[Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
