**Hat of Disguise**

:** This normal-appearing hat contains a powerful enchantment that allows its wearer to alter his appearance as follows:

*Height:* +/-25% of actual height*Weight:* +/-50% of actual weight*Sex:* Male or female*Hair:* Any color*Eyes:* Any color*Complexion:* Any color*Facial features:* Highly mutableThus, the wearer could appear as a comely woman, a half-orc, or possibly even a gnome. If the hat is removed, the disguise is instantly dispelled. The headgear can be used over and over. Note that the hat can be changed (as part of a disguise) to appear as a comb, ribbon, head band, fillet, cap, coif, hood, helmet, etc.
