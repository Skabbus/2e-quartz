**Wand of Paralyzation:** This wand shoots forth a thin ray of bluish color to a maximum range of 60 feet. Any creature touched by the ray must roll successful saving throw vs. wand or be rendered rigidly immobile for 5d4 rounds. A save indicates the ray missed, and there is no effect. As soon as the ray touches one creature, it stops葉he wand can attack only one target per round. The wand has an initiative modifier of +3 , and each use costs one charge. The wand may operate once per round. It may be recharged.
