How to Make a Morale Check

[[Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 49- Morale Ratings|Table 49]] lists the base morale number for various types of creatures. [[Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 50- Situational Modifiers|Table 50]] lists conditions and situations that can modify this base morale number. To roll a morale check, find the rating that most closely matches the creature. Add or subtract the modifiers that apply to the situation. Some modifiers, such as the number of Hit Dice can be calculated in advance. Roll 2d10.

If the total rolled on the dice is equal to or less than the morale rating, the creature is unaffected and keeps fighting. If the roll is greater, the creature panics and flees, or it takes some other appropriate action.
