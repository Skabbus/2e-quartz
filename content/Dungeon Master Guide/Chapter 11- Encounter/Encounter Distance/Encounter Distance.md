Encounter Distance

Once an encounter occurs, it is necessary to know the range at which the creatures might first be noticed. This distance is dependent first on whether or not either group is surprised or, if no surprise occurs, on the type of terrain the encounter occurs in. Encounter distances for different conditions and terrains are listed on [[Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 58- Encounter Distance|Table 58]] .[[Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
