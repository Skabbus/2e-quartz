Planned Encounters

A planned encounter is one of the DM has prepared in advance, one tied to a specific place, event, or condition. These can be divided into [[Dungeon Master Guide/Chapter 11- Encounter/Planned Encounters/Keys|keys]] and [[Dungeon Master Guide/Chapter 11- Encounter/Planned Encounters/Triggers|triggers]] .[[Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
