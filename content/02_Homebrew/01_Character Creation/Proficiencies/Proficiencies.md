# 1. Proficiencies
***
Proficiencies are divided into two groups: *Weapon proficiencies* and *Non-weapon Proficiencies*. The number of Proficiencies a character has access to is determined by level and class, and additional Non-weapon Proficiencies are determined by the Intelligence ability.

## Weapon Proficiencies
***

Weapon competency stretches across 7 levels: 
*Nonproficient, Familiar, Proficient, Specialized, Mastery, High Mastery, Grand Mastery.*

*Specialization* is only available to Warrior classes and *Mastery* are only accessible for single-classed fighters. The highest level of proficiency for other classes is *Proficient*. Each level of proficiency costs one Weapon Proficiency slot. 

An individual weapon proficiency slot can be assigned to a particular weapon (see [[Table 6- Master Weapons Chart|Master Weapons Chart]]). A weapon may however be part of a *weapon tight group*, which in turn is part of a *weapon broad group.* 

The player may elect to invest one weapon proficiency point to a specific weapon, which will grant *familiarity* with the other weapons within the *tight group.* A player may also invest two weapon proficiency points into a *tight group*, which grants proficiency with all weapons within said tight group. Finally, a player may also invest three weapon proficiency slots into a weapon *broad group*, gaining proficiency with all weapons within said broad group. See [[Table 49- Weapon Groups]]. These also apply retroactively, so spending two proficiency slots on different individual weapons within a tight group will grant tight group proficiency, and spending three total weapon proficiency slots within a broad group will grant broad group proficiency.

## Non-weapon Proficiencies
***
Non-weapon proficiency slots are gained based on level and class, with additional proficiency slots based on intelligence of the character. 

Each class has different proficiency groups they can acquire non-weapon proficiencies from at the cost on 1 NWP-slot (unless otherwise stated). These groups can be seen on [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Tables/Table 38- Nonweapon Proficiency Group Crossovers|Table 38- Nonweapon Proficiency Group Crossovers]]:

| **Class**   | **Proficiency Groups**          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------- |
| Fighter     | Warrior, General                |
| Paladin     | Warrior, Priest, General        |
| Ranger      | Warrior, Wizard, General        |
| Cleric      | Priest, General                 |
| Druid       | Priest, Warrior, General        |
| Mage        | Wizard, General                 |
| Illusionist | Wizard, General                 |
| Thief       | Rogue, General                  |
| Bard        | Rogue, Warrior, Wizard, General |

A proficiency can be acquired from outside the specific class Proficiency Group, but the cost increases with +1 NWP-slot to acquire the proficiency. 

Some Non-weapon proficiencies, such as *Blind fighting* or *Mountaineering*, may provide a blank ability for the player and contain no Proficiency Checks on their own. Other Non-weapon proficiencies, such as *Local History* or *Artistic Ability* may be applied to only a certain area (for example, Artistic Ability may be dedicated to painting, sculpting or something else entirely). Finally, some Non-weapon Proficiencies such as *Fire Building* will just allow the player to make a check for the activity. 

A proficiency check is made by rolling D20+*Stat*+*Proficiency Bonus*+*Check Modifier*. In general, a result above 21 results in success. The general check modifiers are listed in the table, but certain circumstances as described in the proficiency and as decided by the DM may change the modifier.

Multiple Non-weapon proficiency slots may be invested in the same Non-weapon proficiency, each one within the same area granting a +2. In the case where the NWP is applied to a sub-category, (i.e. the Artistic Ability example above), the player decides whether to further specialize within the area or expand into a new area (Artistic Ability: Painting at +4 or Painting at +2, Sculpting +2).
