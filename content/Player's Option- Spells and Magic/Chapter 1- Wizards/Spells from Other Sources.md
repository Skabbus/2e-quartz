Spells from Other Sources

While the material on creating wizard characters is updated for this book, you’ll find that spells that appeared in previous books have not been altered. As long as the DM approves, a player character wizard can learn spells from any source the player has available. In fact, the spell lists contained in [[Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells by School/Universal Magic|Appendix 3]] of this book include spells from the [Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01790.htm) *,* *[Tome of Magic](..\TM\DD04161.htm)*, and *The Complete Wizard’s Handbook* as well as dozens of new spells introduced in this book. Additional spells from the *Wizard’s Spell Compendium, Pages from the Mages*, or any other source can be approved by the DM on a case-by-case basis.
