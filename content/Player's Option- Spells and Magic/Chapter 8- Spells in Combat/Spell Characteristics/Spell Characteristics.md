Spell Characteristics

If you’ve played the AD&D game for any amount of time, you are already quite familiar with a number of spell characteristics such as range, duration, area of effect, and other such attributes. Some characteristics are fixed values, while others may vary with the caster’s level. *Player’s Option: Spells & Magic* introduces the new characteristics of **casting subtlety** and [[Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Chapter 8- Spells in Combat/Sensory Signature/Sensory Signature|sensory signature]] , which describe the obviousness of a spell’s casting or effects.

In addition to perception characteristics, we’ll also take a look at spells that may create knockdowns (a concept from the *Player’s Option:* *[Combat & Tactics](..\CT\DD02462.htm)

*book), as well as spells that require attack rolls on the part of the caster.
