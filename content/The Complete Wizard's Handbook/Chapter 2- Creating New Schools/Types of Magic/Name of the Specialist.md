**Name of the Specialist**

Specialists usually derive their names from the names of their schools by changing the suffix. Thus, from *abjuration* comes the *abjurer*, and from *illusion* comes the *illusionist*. Most of the time, this technique is satisfactory.

However, some schools simply do not lend themselves to wizardly-sounding names. For instance, the word *alterer* is clumsy, so the school of alteration has opted for the less awkward *transmuter* for the name of their specialist. We have a similar problem with our school of metamorphics-- both *metamorphist* and *metamorpher* are clumsy. Returning to the thesaurus, we find the word *transfigure*.
