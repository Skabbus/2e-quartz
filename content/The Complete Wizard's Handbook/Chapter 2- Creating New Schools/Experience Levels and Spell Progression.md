**Experience Levels and Spell Progression**

All existing schools use the experience level progression and spell progression given on [Tables 20](..\PHB\DD01470.htm) and [21](..\PHB\DD01471.htm) on page 30 of the *Player's Handbook*. New schools should use these, too. It is possible to devise new tables from scratch, but this creates considerable work for the DM, adding unnecessary complications and an increased chance of an unbalanced campaign.
