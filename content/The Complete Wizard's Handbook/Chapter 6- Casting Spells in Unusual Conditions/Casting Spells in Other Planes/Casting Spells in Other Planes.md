**Casting Spells in Other Planes**

As described on page 132 of the*[Dungeon Master's Guide](..\DMG\DD00660.htm)**,* the AD&D® game world consists of many planes of existence aside from the familiar Prime Material plane. Just as physical laws operate differently in these planes, so do the effects of magic and the ways in which spells are cast. General guidelines for using wizardly magic in the various planes are given below. (If planning a campaign in one or more of the other planes of existence, or if a DM is interested in additional information about the planes, consult the *Manual of the Planes*.)[[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]**
