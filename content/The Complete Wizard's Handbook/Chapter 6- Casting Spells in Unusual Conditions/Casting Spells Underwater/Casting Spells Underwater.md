**Casting Spells Underwater**

Assuming that the wizard can find a way to breathe underwater for extended periods (such as a *water breathing* spell or potion), most of his spells will function normally underwater, with identical casting times, ranges, components, and effects. There are, however, a number of exceptions, detailed below. Rather than being frustrated by these changes, the wise wizard will study them carefully and use them to his advantage[[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
