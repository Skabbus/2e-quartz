**Ideas for New Personality Types**

The personality types are not confined to the ones suggested above. Here are a few other possibilities:· The Sidekick (a trusted companion and assistant to a player character).· The Compromiser (a superb negotiator, dedicated to preserving harmony and good relations among his companions).· The Coward (a reluctant fighter, frightened of everything and always expecting the worst).· The Carefree Wanderer (a happy-go-lucky wayfarer).· The Sneak (a wizard who loves to trick people and take advantage of their naivete).· The Chronicler (a historian devoted to making a permanent, detailed record of his party's activities)[[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
