**A Note About Reaction Adjustments**

Several of the wizard kits receive reaction bonuses and penalties as part of their special benefits and special hindrances. As noted in the [Player's Handbook](..\PHB\DD01436.htm) (page 18), characters with very high or very low Charisma receive "reaction adjustments. " A character with high Charisma gets a bonus (expressed as a plus, such as +3), while a character with low Charisma gets a penalty (expressed as a minus, such as -3).

When rolling 2d10 for encounter reactions (see page 103 in the [Dungeon Master's Guide](..\DMG\DD00564.htm)), do not add the bonus or subtract the penalty from the die roll. If the character has a Charisma of 16, thus receiving a +5 reaction adjustment, subtract that number from the die roll-- do not add it.
