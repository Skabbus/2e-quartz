**1st-Level Bonus Spell**

A 1st-level specialist begins with two spells, but a 1st-level mage begins with only one. This difference is inconsequential over the course of a long campaign, but it can be significant if playing a short adventure with low-level characters[[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
