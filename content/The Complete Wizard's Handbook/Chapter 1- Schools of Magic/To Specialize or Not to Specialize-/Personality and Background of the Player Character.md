**Personality and Background of the Player Character**

Some aspect of the player character's personality might suggest whether he's best suited for the life of a mage or that of a specialist. An impulsive, overeager character might lack the patience for the studious life of a specialist. Conversely, a thoughtful, scholarly character might find the life of a mage too confining. A character who comes from a long line of mages might want to continue the family tradition. A character whose brother was killed by an evil specialist NPC might want to study the same school to prepare himself for a confrontation with the murderer[[The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
