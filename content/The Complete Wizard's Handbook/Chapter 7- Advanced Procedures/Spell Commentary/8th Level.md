**8th Level**

Sink**This spell is a terrific way to help a character who is on the verge of falling off the edge of a cliff or losing his grip when hanging from the edge of a building. While the subject clings to the cliff, the wizard casts *sink.* If the spell is successfully cast, the subject becomes stuck to the cliff and won't fall. However, the spell expires in four turns, at which time the subject risks falling.**
