**Cutlass**

The cutlass is a sword with a single-edged, curved, broad blade attached to a basket hilt. The blade is short and heavy. The sword is favored among pirate crews and is easily found in port communities, but is rare inland. Cutlass users enjoy the same advantages in Parrying as broad sword users.*"Cutlasses cause damage compared to short swords, but are slower and heavier. A broad sword is a better weapon, since it gives the same Parry benefits and causes more damage.''-- First Mate Arlundar, of the galleon* Angelwing[[00_Rulebook/Arms and Equipment Guide/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
