The *light warhorse* category includes ponies and riding horses (the latter reflects custom and use rather than the ability of the horse to carry actual weight).

*Limits:* A horse can wear half barding one class heavier than its maximum full barding. Flying creatures are limited to [[Leather Barding (AC 6)|leather]] and [[Padded Barding (AC6)#55ad43fc|padded barding]] only.*[[Padded Barding (AC6)#55ad43fc|Padded]]* includes quilted armor.*[[Scale Barding (AC 5)#2fe80346|Scale]]* includes studded leather, [[Brigandine Barding (AC 5)|brigandine]], and [[Ring Mail|ring mail]] (weights will differ).*[[Banded Barding (AC 3)#94383722|Banded]]* includes [[Splint Barding (AC 3)|splint]].
