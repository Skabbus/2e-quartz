**Table 4: Optional Partial Barding**The following chart may be used to determine the armor class of partial barding. Bonuses are added to the mount's base AC.

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Armor** | **Full** | **Chanfron/** | **Cuello/** | **Flanchard/** |
| **Type** | **Barding** | **Poitrel** | **Crinet** | **Crupper** |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leather | 6 | -----------------(see notes)---------------- |
| Scale | 5 | -----------------(see notes)---------------- |

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chain | 4 | \*\* | 0 | \*\* |
| Banded | 3 | \*\* | \*\* | \*\* |
| Plate | 2 | 1 ea. | \*\* | 1 ea. |
| Field Plate | 1 | 1 ea. | \*\* | 1 ea. |
| Full Plate | 0 | 1 ea. | 1 ea. | 1 ea. |

**Notes:***[[Leather Barding (AC 6)|Leather]]**:* The only effective combination less than full leather is half leather, which gives protection to the front of the horse only.*[[Scale Barding (AC 5)#2fe80346|Scale]]**:* Half scale protects the front of the horse only. Any other combination of at least three pieces (except crinet) gives a +1 bonus to armor class.\*\* Any two pieces give a +1 bonus to AC.[[00_Rulebook/Arms and Equipment Guide/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
