**Splint Barding (AC 3)**

Splint barding is similar to [[Banded Barding (AC 3)#94383722|banded barding]]. The main difference is that the strips of metal are vertical instead of horizontal. The weight and protective value are the same at 85 pounds and AC 3. **Half Splint:** This covers only the front half of the horse. Its weight is 60 pounds.
