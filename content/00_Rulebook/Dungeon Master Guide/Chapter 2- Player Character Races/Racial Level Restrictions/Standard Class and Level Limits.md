Standard Class and Level Limits

Before removing or modifying level limits, familiarize yourself with the game and the balances that currently exist. Only after you are experienced and comfortable with these should you begin alteration of the non-human level limits. The standard level limits for all races and classes are given in [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 7- Racial Class and Level Limits|Table 7]] .[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
