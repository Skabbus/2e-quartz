Magical Weapons

To determine the type of magical weapon found, roll once on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 108- Weapon Type (D6)|Table 108]] for a weapon type. Then roll on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 109- Attack Roll Adjustment|Table 109]] to determine the plus (or minus) of the weapon. If a Special result is rolled, roll on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 110- Special Weapons (D10)|Table 110]] to determine the exact weapon found. A range of numbers in parentheses is the number of items found.
