Armor and Shields

To determine the magical item found, roll for the type of armor on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 105- Armor Type|Table 105]] and then the magical adjustment on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 106- Armor Class Adjustment|Table 106]] .
