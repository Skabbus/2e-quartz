Lock Quality

The quality of a lock can increase, decrease, or leave unchanged a thief's chance of picking that lock. The higher the quality of the lock, the harder it is for the thief to pick. [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 24- Lock Quality|Table 24]] lists the different lock qualities and the amount they add or subtract from a thief's percentage chance to open it. Unless otherwise noted, assume that all locks are of good quality.
