Planned Encounters

A planned encounter is one of the DM has prepared in advance, one tied to a specific place, event, or condition. These can be divided into [[Keys|keys]] and [[Triggers|triggers]] .[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
