List of Staves**Staff-Mace:** This clerical weapon appears to be a normal wooden staff of the type used when trekking in the wilderness. This item is typically made of bronzewood, reinforced by heavy bands and tips of iron. It gives off a very faint aura of alteration magic. Upon command, the *staff-mace* takes on one of three forms, as desired by the possessor.
