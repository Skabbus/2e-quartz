**Staff of Power:** The *staff of power* is a very potent magical item, with offensive and defensive abilities. The powers below cost one charge each:**•** *continual light***•** *magic missile or lightning bolt***•** *ray of enfeeblement***•** *levitation***•** *cone of cold or fireball*The following powers drain two charges each:**•** *shield, 5-foot radius***•** *globe of invulnerability***•** *paralyzation\**\* Paralyzation is a ray from the end of the staff extending in a cone 40 feet long and 20 feet wide at the far end.**

The DM may assign alternate powers by random die roll.

The wielder of a *staff of power* gains a +2 bonus to Armor Class and saving throws. He may use the staff to smite opponents. It strikes as a +2 magical weapon and inflicts 1d6+2 points of damage; if one charge is expended, the staff causes double damage, but two charges do not cause triple damage.

A *staff of power* can be broken for a retributive strike (see [[Magi|staff of the magi]] ). The staff can be recharged.
