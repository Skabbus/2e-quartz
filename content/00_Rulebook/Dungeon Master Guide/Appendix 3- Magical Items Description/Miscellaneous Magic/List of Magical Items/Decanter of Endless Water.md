**Decanter of Endless Water**

:** This stoppered flask looks ordinary but radiates the aura of magic. If the stopper is removed, and the proper words spoken, a stream of fresh or salt water pours out, as ordered. There are separate command words for the amount as well as the type of water. Water can be made to come forth as follows:

*Stream:* pours out 1 gallon per round*Fountain:* 5-foot long stream at 5 gallons per round*Geyser:* 20-foot long stream at 30 gallons per roundThe geyser causes considerable back pressure, and the holder must be well braced or be knocked over. The force of the geyser will kill small animals and insects (mice, moles, small bats, etc.). The command word must be given to cease.
