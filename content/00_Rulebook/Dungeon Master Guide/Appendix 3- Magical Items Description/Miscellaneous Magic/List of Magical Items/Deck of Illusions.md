**Deck of Illusions**

:** This set of parchment cards is usually found in an ivory, leather, or wood box. A full deck consists of 34 cards of 4 suits. When a card is drawn at random and thrown to the ground, an illusion with audible and visual components is formed. This lasts until dispelled. The illusionary creature will not go more than 30 feet away from where the card landed, but will otherwise move and act as if it were real. When the *illusion* is dispelled, the card becomes blank and cannot be used again. If the card is picked up, the *illusion* is automatically and instantly dispelled.
