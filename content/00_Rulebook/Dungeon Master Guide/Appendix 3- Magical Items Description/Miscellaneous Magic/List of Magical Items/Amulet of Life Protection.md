**Amulet of Life Protection**

:** This pendant or brooch device serves as protection for the psyche. The wearer is protected from the *magic jar* spell or any similar mental attack that would usurp control of the wearer's body. If the wearer is slain, the psyche enters the amulet and is protected for seven full days. Thereafter, it departs to the plane of its alignment. If the amulet is destroyed during the seven days, the psyche is utterly and irrevocably annihilated.
