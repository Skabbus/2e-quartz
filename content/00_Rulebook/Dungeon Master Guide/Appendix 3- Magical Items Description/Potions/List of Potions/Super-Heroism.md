**Super-Heroism**

:** This potion gives the individual a temporary increase in levels (see [[Heroism|heroism]] potion) if he has fewer than 13 levels of experience. It is similar to the *heroism* potion, but more powerful, and its effects last just 5d6 melee rounds:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level of Consumer** | **Number of Levels Bestowed** | **Additonal Temporary Hit Dice** |
| 0 | 6 | 5d10 |
| 1st-3rd | 5 | 4d10+1 |
| 4th-6th | 4 | 3d10+2 |
| 7th-9th | 3 | 2d10+3 |
| 10th-12th | 2 | 1d10+4 |
