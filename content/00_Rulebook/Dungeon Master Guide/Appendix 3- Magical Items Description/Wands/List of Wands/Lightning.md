**Wand of Lightning:** This wand has two functions that closely resemble wizard spells:**•** *Shock:* This does 1-10 hit points of damage to a target struck in melee combat, with no saving throw. Characters wearing metal armor and/or shields are treated as armor class 10. Plain leather and wood work normally. Magical bonuses on metal armor do not affect Armor Class, but a *ring of protection* does. The shock uses one charge.**•** *Lightning Bolt:* The possessor of the wand can discharge a bolt of lightning. The stroke can be either a forked or straight bolt(see wizard spell,*[lightning bolt](..\PHB\DD01915.htm)*). Damage is 12-36 (6d6, treating 1s as 2s), but a saving throw is applicable. This function uses two charges and has an initiate modifier of +2.

The wand may be recharged. It can perform only one function per round.
