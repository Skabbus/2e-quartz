Weapon Ego

Only after all aspects of a weapon have been determined and recorded can the ego rating of a weapon be found. Ego, along with intelligence, will be a factor with regard to the dominance of weapon over character, as detailed on [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 119- Weapon Ego|Table 119]] .[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
