**Ring of Jumping**

:** The wearer of this ring is able to leap 30 feet ahead or 10 feet backward or straight up, with an arc of about 2 feet for every 10 feet traveled (see the 1st-level wizard spell, [jump](..\PHB\DD01832.htm) ). The wearer must use the ring's power carefully, for it can perform only four times per day.
