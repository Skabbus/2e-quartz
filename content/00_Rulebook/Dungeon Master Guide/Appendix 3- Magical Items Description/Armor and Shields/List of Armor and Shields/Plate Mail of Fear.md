**Plate Mail of Fear**

:** This armor functions as normal *plate mail +1.* However, it is imbued with 2d23 *fear* charges. Upon uttering the command, a charge is spent and the wearer radiates a 30-foot aura of fear. All creatures (except the one wearing the armor) must save vs. spell or flee in panic for 1d4+1 rounds. When all charges are spent, the armor functions as normal *plate mail +1.* It cannot be recharged.
