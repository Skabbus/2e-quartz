**Shield -1, missile attractor**

:** This not only makes the bearer equivalent to a shieldless person, it also attracts missiles of all types to itself: It doubles or triples the bearer's chances of being selected by random die rolling according to the size of the party he is with (see Chapter 9, "[Firing into a Melee](..\PHB\DD01715.htm) "). This cursed shield is not distinguishable from a useful magical shield.
