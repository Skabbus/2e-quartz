Spell Level Range

The level range shown in [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 90- Scrolls (D6)|Table 90]] gives the parameters for random determination of spell level for scrolls if you choose not to set this yourself. With spell level determined, find the particular spell by consulting the appropriate Spell Tables in the *Player's Handbook.*[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
