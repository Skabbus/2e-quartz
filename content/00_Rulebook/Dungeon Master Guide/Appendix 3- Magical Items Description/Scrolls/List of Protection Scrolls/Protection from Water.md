**Protection from Water**

:** (Reading time—6) This protection extends in a 10-foot diameter sphere centered on the reader. All forms of water—liquid, solid, and vapor, ice, hail, snow, sleet, steam, and so forth—are unable to penetrate the sphere of protection. If those protected come upon a form of water, the substance simply will not touch them; thus, they will not slip on ice, sink into a body of water, etc. The protection lasts for 1d4+4 turns.
