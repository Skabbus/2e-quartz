**Protection from Poison**

:** (Reading time—3) The protection afforded by this scroll extends only to the reader. No form of poison—ingested, contacted, breathed, etc.—will affect the protected individual, and any poison in the reader's system is permanently neutralized. The protection otherwise lasts 1d10+2 rounds.
