**Sword, Cursed Berserking:** This performs by every test, save that of the heat of battle, as a +2 magical sword of some sort. However, in actual battle its wielder will go berserk, attacking the nearest creature and continuing to fight until dead or until no living thing remains within 60 feet. The sword has a +2 bonus and otherwise acts as a *cursed sword +1.* The possessor of a *cursed berserking sword* can be rid of it only if it is exorcised via a *remove curse* spell or *wish.*[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
