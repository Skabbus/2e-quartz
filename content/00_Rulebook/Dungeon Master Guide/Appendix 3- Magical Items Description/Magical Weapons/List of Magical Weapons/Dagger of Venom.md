**Dagger of Venom**

:** This appears to be a standard *dagger +1,* but its hilt holds a hidden store of poison. Any hit on a roll of 20 injects fatal poison into the opponent unless a saving throw vs. poison is successful. The *dagger of venom* holds up to six doses of poison. If the hilt contains fewer than six doses, the owner can pour more in up to the maximum. (Use of this weapon by good—particularly lawful good—characters must be carefully monitored for effects on alignment.)[[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Table of Contents#dc24ea2a|Table of Contents]]
