Treasure Tables

To simplify the assignment of treasures to lairs and monsters, the AD&D® game uses a set of alphabetic codes to categorize different sizes and types of treasure. Each monster listing in the *Monstrous Compendium* has a "Treasure Type" listing followed by a series of letters. These letters refer to [[00_Rulebook/Dungeon Master Guide/Tables/Table 84- Treasure Types#0ceeca52|Table 84]] in Appendix 1 of the *DMG.*
