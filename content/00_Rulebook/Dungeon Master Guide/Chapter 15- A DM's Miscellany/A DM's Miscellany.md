**Chapter 15:**

**A DM's Miscellany**The previous chapters have presented a lot of rules and covered a lot of ground, but there are always a few things that don't fit into neat little categories (or even big categories!). Some of these are situations that arise all the time during adventures. Others are situations or background facts you will need only occasionally. These "left-overs," common and uncommon, are discussed below.
