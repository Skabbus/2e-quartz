Titles, Offices, and Positions

Alderman: A town or city officialAle-conner: Official who tests and approves all ales and cidersAnchorite: A religious hermitBailiff: A sergeant or commander of the guardBeadle: A messenger of the law courtsBurgomaster: A town or city officialCatchpoll: A commander of the guardChamberlain: Overseer of a household, office or courtCommon-weigher: Town official who checks merchants' weights and measuresConstable: A commander of the local guardCouncilor: A town or city official or an advisor of the courtCustoms agent: One responsible for collecting the taxes on all imports and exports.
