**Musical Items**

Baton:** The slender stick used by conductors to direct a performance.**

**Metronome:** A device that produces regularly timed beats.

**Mute:** A device that muffles the sound of an instrument.

**Plectrum:** General name for any device used to pluck the strings of a musical instrument.

**Podium:** Raised platform on which the conductor stands.
