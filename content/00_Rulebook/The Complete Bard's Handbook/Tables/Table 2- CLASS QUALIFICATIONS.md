**Qualifications**Generating ability scores high enough to qualify for the bard class is difficult. In fact, it is unlikely unless your DM allows one of the [alternative dice-rolling methods](..\PHB\DD01423.htm) described in Chapter 1 of the *Player's Handbook*. A complete discussion on generating bard player characters (along with pre-generated bards) using all six dice-rolling methods is presented at the end of this section.

To be a bard or not to be a bard, these are the qualifications (see Table 2).

**Table 2: CLASS QUALIFICATIONSAbility Requirements:**

**Dexterity 12Intelligence 13Charisma 15**Prime Requisites\*:** Dexterity, Charisma**Races Allowed:** Human, Half-elf**Alignments Allowed:** Any Neutral(LN, NG, N, NE, CN)\*Bards with a 16 or better in both Dexterity and Charisma gain a 10% bonus to their awarded experience points.**
