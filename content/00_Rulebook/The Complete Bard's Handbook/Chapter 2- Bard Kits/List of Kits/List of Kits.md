**List of Kits**

All of the bard kits are listed on the following pages. The first kit is that of the True Bard, as presented in the [Player's Handbook](..\PHB\DD01508.htm). This kit establishes a base from which all the other kits are built. The other kits are arranged in alphabetical order. A section on multi-classed bards follows. It contains an additional four kits, but these are restricted to demihumans.
