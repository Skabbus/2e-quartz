## Poetry

(Proficiency)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Poetry |
| Group(s) | General |
| Ability | Intelligence |
| Modifier | -2 |
| Slots | 1 |

This proficiency includes the skills necessary to recite poetry and judge its quality. It also indicates that a character has a repertoire of poems memorized for recital at any time. No check is required for a normal recital.

If the character can read and write, original poems can be written. A successful check indicates that the poem is of above average quality. [[Humanoid Proficiencies|Proficiency List]] [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Book of Humanoids/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
