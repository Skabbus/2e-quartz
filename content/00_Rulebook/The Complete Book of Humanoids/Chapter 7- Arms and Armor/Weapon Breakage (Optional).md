## Weapon Breakage

(Optional Rule)

Humanoids wielding weapons of their own make need not check for breakage. When large-sized humanoids use human or demihuman weapons, the weapon may break under their greater strength and force. This chance is read directly from the attack roll. Use [[Arms and Armor#Table_6:_Optional_Weapon_Breakage|Table 6]] to determine if the weapon breaks. Weapon Size refers to the weapon's size classification. Roll is the number or less on the 1d20 attack roll that will break the weapon (excluding strength adjustments). A broken weapon becomes useless immediately upon breaking. A magical weapon receives a saving throw vs. crushing blow to avoid breaking.

#### Table 6: Optional Weapon Breakage

| Weapon Size | Roll |
| --- | --- |
| S | 10 |
| M | 5 |
| L | 2 |
