**Chapter 5: Proficiencies**It's strongly suggested that you incorporate the optional proficiency rules into your campaign, especially if you're using the character kits from Chapter 4. This chapter presents all of the nonweapon proficiencies relevant to the ranger from the *Player's Handbook*, along with descriptions of a few new ones and clarifications of some old ones.
