**Role-Playing**The *AD&D®* game isn't all combat and magic, and so a warrior doesn't have to be just a collection of numbers which responds to situations by killing everything in sight and collecting coins. No, there's much more to role-playing than that, and this chapter is devoted to role-playing the warrior character.
