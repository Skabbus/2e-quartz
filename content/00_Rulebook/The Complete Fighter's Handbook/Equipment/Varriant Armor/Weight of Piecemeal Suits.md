**Weight of Piecemeal Suits**

To calculate the weight of a piecemeal suit of armor, follow these guidelines:

The **breastplate** is 1/2 the weight of the original suit.

Each **arm** and **leg** is 1/8 the weight of the original suit.

**About Magical Armor**If a suit of magical armor is used for one of these piecemeal efforts, it grants none of its magical bonus; once the magical armor is split into little bits, or pieces are merely separated and not worn together, the magical bonus doesn't work.
