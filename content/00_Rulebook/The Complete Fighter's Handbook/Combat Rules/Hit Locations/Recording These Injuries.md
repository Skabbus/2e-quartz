**Recording These Injuries**

It's simple to record these injuries.

You don't record the damage separately. All the hit-point losses are applied against the character's normal pool of hit points, just as before.

On the Combat Sheet provided at the end of this chapter, or on a scratch-sheet, you'll want to record any special combat results and how long they're in effect. For example: "Right arm useless until combat over."[[00_Rulebook/The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
