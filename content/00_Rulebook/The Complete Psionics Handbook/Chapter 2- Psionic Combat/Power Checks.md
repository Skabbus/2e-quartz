## Power Checks

The power check is fundamental to psionic combat. In general, no psionic power functions unless the character first makes a successful power check ([[A Psionics Primer|Chapter 1 explains how the check is made]]). Modifiers apply for some powers. Furthermore, several powers require an additional power check before they'll yield a specific result. Check the individual power descriptions in Chapters 3-8 for details.
