**Know Direction**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Clairsentience **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Int **Initial Cost:** 1 **Maintenance Cost:** na **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneThe psionicist becomes his own compass. By paying 1 PSP and making a successful power check, he knows which way is north.**

*Power Score* - The power is automatically maintained for one day.

*20* - The psionicist is disoriented; he cannot use this power again for 1d6 hours.
