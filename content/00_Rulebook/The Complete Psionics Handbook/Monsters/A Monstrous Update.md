## A Monstrous Update

Many creatures in the AD&DÂ® *Monstrous Compendium* series boast psionic powers. The text below brings those monsters up to date with the rules presented in this book. The creatures appear in alphabetical order (creatures from the Outer Planes are listed separately). Each entry is organized as follows:

**Level**-How tough the monster is, in terms of a psionicist's experience level.

**Dis/Sci/Dev**-How many disciplines the creature can access, followed by the total number of sciences and devetions the creature knows (in all accessible disciplines).

**Attack/Defense**-Telepathic attack and defense modes the creature can use. (Defense modes are not included in the total number of powers the creature knows.) Abbreviations are as follows:

| Attack Modes | Defense Mode |
| --- | --- |
| PB = [[Psionic Blast\|Psionic Blast]] | M- = [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Mind Blank\|Mind Blank]] |
| MT = [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Devotions-/Mind Thrust\|Mind Thurst]] | TS = [[Thought Shield\|Thought Shield]] |
| EW = [[Ego Whip\|Ego Whip]] | MB = [[Mental Barrier\|Mental Barrier]] |
| II = [[Id Insinuation\|Id Insinuation]] | IF = [[Intellect Fortress\|Intellect Fortress]] |
| PsC = [[Psychic Crush\|Psychic Crush]] | TW = [[Tower of Iron Will\|Tower of Iron Will]] |

**Power Score:** The creature's typical score when using a power that is not automatically successful. **PSPs:** The creature's total pool of psionic strength points (the maximum available to it). **Type:** The general type of powers the creature can use. Powers listed after this entry (if any) are representative; the list is not necessarily complete. ***\*** Creatures always knows a power that is distinguished by an asterisk.*
