**Enhancement**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Metapsionics **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Wis -3 **Initial Cost:** 30 **Maintenance Cost:** 8/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 5 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** 6th levelThis power yields the same results as the Meditative Focus proficiency, in less time. When a psionicist learns this power, he selects one discipline to enhance. As long as he maintains the enhancement power, all of his power scores within the chosen discipline are increased by two. At the same time, all other power scores are reduced by one.**

*Power Score* - Other scores are not reduced.

*20* - All power scores in the chosen discipline are reduced by one for 24 hours.
