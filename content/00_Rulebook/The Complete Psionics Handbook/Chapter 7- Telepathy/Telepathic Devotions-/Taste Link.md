Taste link allows the telepath to tap into the flavor senses of another person or creature. The psionicist tastes whatever his link tastes.**

If the linked creature takes poison orally, the telepath must make a saving throw vs. poison to avoid passing out and severing the link. The psionicist can't actually be poisoned this way, however.

*Power Score* - Scent link is also gained (not a defined power).

*20* - The psionicist has a bitter taste in his mouth, but no other effect occurs.
