**Telempathic Projection**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Telepathy **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Wis -2 **Initial Cost:** contact **Maintenance Cost:** 4/round **Range:** Unlimited **Preparation Time:** 1 **Area of Effect:** 10 yard diam. **Prerequisites:** [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Psionics Handbook/Chapter 7- Telepathy/Telepathic Sciences/Mindlink|Mindlink]], [[Contact|Contact]]Telempathic projection allows the user to send emotions to everyone who has been contacted within a common 10-yard diameter. This power cannot radically change a character's emotional state, however. A character who is very angry could be made only slightly angry, for example, but not happy. Empathic changes are a matter of degrees, no more.**

*Power Score* - Emotions can be drastically altered (love to hatred, etc.) while this power is maintained.

*20* - All affected characters experience strong negative emotions toward the psionicist for 1d6 rounds.
