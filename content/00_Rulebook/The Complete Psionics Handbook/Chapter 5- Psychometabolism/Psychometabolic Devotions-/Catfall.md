**Catfall**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Dex -2 **Initial Cost:** 4 **Maintenance Cost:** na **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneA character using this power can spring like a cat in the same round, and always land gracefully on his feet. He still suffers damage from falling, but the damage is halved. He can jump down 30 feet without risking any injury at all.**

*Power Score* - The psionicist can jump 50 feet instead of 30 during this single use of the power.

*20* - No effect.
