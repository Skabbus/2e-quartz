**Body Weaponry**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con -3 **Initial Cost:** 9 **Maintenance Cost:** 4/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** Personal **Prerequisites:** NoneBody weaponry allows the psionicist to convert one of his arms into a weapon. Virtually any sort of weapon can be imitated - except a short bow, hand crossbow, light crossbow, or any weapon the psionicist cannot normally use. The arm actually becomes wood and/or metal, and assumes the weapon's form. It behaves in every respect like a normal weapon of the chosen type, with a bonus: it can never be dropped or stolen.**

*Power Score* - The "armament" gives the psionicist a +1 attack bonus (but no damage bonus).

*20* - The psionicist must make a system shock roll or pass out for 1d10 rounds.
