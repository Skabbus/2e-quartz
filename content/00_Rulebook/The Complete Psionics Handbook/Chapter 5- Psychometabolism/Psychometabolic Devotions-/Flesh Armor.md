**Flesh Armor**

Psionic Power** **Discipline:** Psychometabolism **Power:** Minor (Devotion) **Power Score:** Con -3 **Initial Cost:** 8 **Maintenance Cost:** 4/round **Range:** 0 **Preparation Time:** 0 **Area of Effect:** personal **Prerequisites:** noneThe psionicist transforms his own skin into armor. No one can see the change, but it's as if he had actually acquired some type of armor. The type of armor his body mimics depends on the result of his power check, as shown in the following table:**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | left hand functions as a shield, AC 9 |
| 2 | leather, AC 8 |
| 3-4 | ring mail, AC 7 |
| 5-6 | scale mail, AC 6 |
| 7-8 | chain mail, AC 5 |
| 9-10 | banded mail, AC 4 |
| 11+ | plate mail, AC 3 |

Because this armor is part of his body, the psionicist can enjoy its benefits without suffering any penalty he might have if he were actually wearing that type of armor. The armor created by this power weighs nothing, has no magnetic properties, and in no way encumbers the psionicist.

*Power Score* - The psionicist gains a +1 bonus to the armor class listed above.

*20* - The power just grows ugly hair over his body, which must be shaved off or he loses 2 points of Charisma.
