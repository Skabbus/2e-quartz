Contents

This introduction describes how to interpret the monsters in this book. In addition, there are three small appendices in the back of the book. The first deals with [[Instructions for the Blank Monster Form|making monsters]] . The second covers [[00_Rulebook/Monstrous Manual/Appendix II- Monster Summoning Tables/Monster Summoning I|monster summoning]] and includes tables for random determination of summoned creatures; to make random encounter charts for a campaign, the DM should refer to [Chapter 11](..\DMG\DD00536.htm) of the *DMG*. The third appendix is concerned with [[NPC Parties|creating NPC parties]] .[[Monstrous Manual Index#6d2de9d5|Index]]
