**Chapter 4: Paladin Kits** Kits are collections of proficiencies, benefits, and hindrances that distinguish one character of the same class from another. A kit also provides information about the character's personality, background, and roles. It's not necessary to use kits, but they make characters more fun to play and add color and depth to a campaign.
