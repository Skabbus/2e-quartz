**Chapter 5: Proficiencies** We strongly recommend using nonweapon proficiencies in your campaign, particularly if you're incorporating the character kits from Chapter 4. This chapter lists all of the paladin's nonweapon proficiencies from the *PH*, describes a few new proficiencies, and clarifies some old ones.
