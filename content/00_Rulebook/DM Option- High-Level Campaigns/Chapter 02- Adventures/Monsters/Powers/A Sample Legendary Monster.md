A Sample Legendary Monster

An elder gorgon might have the following statistics (without adjustments for ability scores):

**Elder Gorgon:** AC –5; MV 17 (enhanced movement power); HD 23; hp 105; THAC0 –3; #AT 3; Dmg 2d6+6/2d6+6 (enhanced melee damage power); SA petrification breath; SD immune to blunt weapons, effected only by magical weapons of +2 or better (physical invulnerability power, twice), immune to earth-based attacks, including petrification (magical invulnerability power); MR 80%; SZ H (10’ tall at the shoulder); ML average (10); Int animal (1); AL N; XP 24,000.

Notes: SA—Four times per day, the elder gorgon can breath a cone of petrification 85’ long, 5’ in diameter at the base, and 30’ in diameter at the far end. All creatures within the area of effect are turned to stone unless they make a successful saving throw versus petrification at –5 (breath weapon at great elder rank).
