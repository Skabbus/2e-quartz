**Chapter 7:**

High-Level Characters**[[Think on an Epic Scale|Chapter 1]] explained that high-level characters are unusual and heroic. This chapter includes rules for giving high-level characters abilities that allow them to function as epic heroes that are truly different from lesser mortals. It also explains some limitations that even epic heroes must face.
