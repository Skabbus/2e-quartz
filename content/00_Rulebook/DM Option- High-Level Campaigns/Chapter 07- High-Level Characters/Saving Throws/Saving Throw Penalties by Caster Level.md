Saving Throw Penalties by Caster Level

Using [[00_Rulebook/DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Tables/Table 36- Saving Throws by Caster Level or Hit Dice|Table 36]] can be somewhat cumbersome to use in regular play because the DM must perform a calculation each time a spell is cast. Table 36 is intended primarily for magical dueling (see [[Magical Duels in the Campaign|Chapter 5]]).

A quicker and easier method to adjust saving throws by caster level is to assign a penalty based on the caster’s level. [[00_Rulebook/DM Option- High-Level Campaigns/Tables/Table 38- Saving Throw Penalties by Caster Level|Table 38]] lists the penalties. If you use Table 38, do not use Table 36; use Table 60 from the [Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01724.htm)instead.
