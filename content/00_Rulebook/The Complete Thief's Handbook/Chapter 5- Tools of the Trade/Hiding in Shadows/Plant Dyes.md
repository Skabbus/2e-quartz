**Plant Dyes**

Analogous to the use of charcoal in dim lighting, a thief can use plant dyes (crushed leaves, berries, certain oily barks, etc.) to disguise his exposed face and hands and add a further +2% to his hide in shadows chance in suitable outdoor settings. Such plant materials are usually rather sticky and smelly, however![[00_Rulebook/The Complete Thief's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
