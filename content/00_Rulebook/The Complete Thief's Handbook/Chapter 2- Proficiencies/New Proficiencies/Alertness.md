**Alertness**

1 slot, Wisdom, +1 modifier.

*Required:* Burglar.

*Recommended:* All.

A character with this proficiency is able to instinctively notice and recognize signs of a disturbance in the immediate vicinity, reducing by 1 in 10 the character's chance of being surprised whenever he makes a successful proficiency check.
