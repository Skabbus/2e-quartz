**Special Social Factors**

These include racial divisions, religious persecution of thieves, unique historical factors, and the like. The DM should note any of these which are important. Since such factors will always be unique, there is no way the design system here can accommodate them. The DM will have to ascribe modifiers to certain dice rolls for these factors as he sees fit![[00_Rulebook/The Complete Thief's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
