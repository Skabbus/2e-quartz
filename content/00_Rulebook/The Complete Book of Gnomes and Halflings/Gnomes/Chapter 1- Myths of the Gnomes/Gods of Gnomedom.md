**Gods Of Gnomedom**

Gnomes are not a tremendously devout folk, but they do have a well-developed pantheon of deities, and they like to honor their gods with frequent "observances"--rites that tend to be performed, naturally, as wild and raucous festivals. The major gods listed below are described in more details on pages 33-37 and 71 of DMGR4, *Monster Mythology* .[[00_Rulebook/The Complete Book of Gnomes and Halflings/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
