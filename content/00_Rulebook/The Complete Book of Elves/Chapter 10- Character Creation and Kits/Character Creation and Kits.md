If characters may be warriors, wizards, priests, thieves, or multiclass combinations. If they are multiclassed, they may select from fighter/mage, fighter/thief, mage/thief, or fighter/mage/thief kits. If a player chooses a multiclass kit, he or she should be familiar with the abilities and limitations of the classes in the [Player's Handbook](..\PHB\DD01513.htm). The character's abilities will be further modified by subrace, as noted in this chapter**. Note:** Elf PCs may become bards *only* if they adopt the Elven Minstrel Kit.
