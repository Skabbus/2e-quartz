**Appendix 4: Priest Spells by Sphere**In the [PHB](..\PHB\DD01480.htm) , clerics have major access to all, astral, charm, combat, creation, divination, guardian, healing, necromantic, protection, summoning, sun, and minor access to elemental.

**Revision: Clerics lose access to sun, elemental air, and elemental fire. They retain minor access to elemental water and elemental earth.** Previously, druids had major access to all, animal, elemental, healing, plant, weather, and minor access to divination.

**Revision: Druids lose access to the sphere of divination. They gain major access to the sphere of sun.**\* Cooperative magic spells. **Bold** spells appear in the *Tome of Magic*. *Italicized* spells are reversible. Underlined spells are introduced in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Appendix 2- New Priest Spells/New Priest Spells|Appendix 2]] of this book. As mentioned in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Chapter 3- Other Spellcasters/Other Spellcasters|Chapter 3]] , some spells have been added to the sphere of all. Also, other spheres have been reorganized.
