Other Spellcasters

Wizards and priests aren’t the only spellcasters in the AD&D game; [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Chapter 6- Magic/Other Spellcasters/Paladins|paladins]] , [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Chapter 6- Magic/Other Spellcasters/Rangers|rangers]] , and [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Spells and Magic/Chapter 6- Magic/Other Spellcasters/Bards|bards]] also have magical abilities. These are represented by spell point progression tables similar to those of the previous sections, although these characters don’t acquire their spell powers until a little bit later in their careers.
