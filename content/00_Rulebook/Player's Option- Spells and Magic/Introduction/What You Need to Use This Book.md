What You Need to Use This Book

At a minimum, you should have access to a [Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01421.htm)and [Dungeon Master](..\DMG\DD00190.htm) *® Guide* to make use of this rules expansion. Note that *Player’s Option: Spells & Magic* is compatible with the previous books in the Player’s Option line; character class design rules in this book are expansions of the class design rules from [Skills & Powers](..\SP\DD03003.htm) , and the chapter on spells in combat is tailored for use with the [Combat & Tactics](..\CT\DD02591.htm) skirmish system. In addition, the [Tome of Magic](..\TM\DD04124.htm)is referred to a number of times in this book; you don’t need *Tome of Magic* to use this book, but it adds many spells and magical items you may find useful in your campaign.
