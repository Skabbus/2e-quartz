Charisma Charisma is split into the subabilities of [[Charisma, Leadership, Appearance#c93613cd|Leadership]] , which measures forcefulness of personality and how willing others are to follow the character’s lead, and [[Charisma, Leadership, Appearance#f1d2b908|Appearance]] , which gauges physical attractiveness, presence, and poise. Leon decides his character will have these Leadership and Appearance scores:

Leadership

A character with a high Leadership often may be a group’s leader, or at least its spokesman. Generals and those who can calm or incite a mob with a few words all have good Leadership scores.

**Loyalty Base:** This modifier is applied to henchmen’s loyalty scores (see the [*Dungeon Master® Guide*](..\DMG\DD00589.htm)). This modifier can be crucial during battles, where good morale is vital.

**Max. # of Henchmen:** This is the maximum number of permanent allies and retainers a character can attract. This does not affect the number of hirelings, mercenaries, or other servitors a character can have.

**Proficiencies:** This score is used as the base number for such nonweapon proficiencies as gaming and disguise.

Appearance

This determines the physical presence and attractiveness of the character. A character with a high Appearance score would be handsome or beautiful, perhaps even famous for outstanding looks (such as Helen of Troy).

**Reaction Adjustment:** This number modifies the Reaction Roll made when a character interacts with NPCs and intelligent creatures for the first time (see the [DMG](..\DMG\DD00563.htm)). Obnoxious behavior can negate bonuses for a high Appearance score, just as solicitous manners can overcome a weakness.

**Proficiencies:** This score acts as the base number for proficiencies such as dancing and etiquette.
