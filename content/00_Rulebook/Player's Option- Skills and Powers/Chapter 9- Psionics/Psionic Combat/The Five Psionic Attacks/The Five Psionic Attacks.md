The Five Psionic Attacks

The five psionic attack forms *are* *[[Ego Whip (EW)|ego whip]]* *,* *[[Id Insinuation (II)|id insinuation]]* *,* *[[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 9- Psionics/Psionic Combat/The Five Psionic Attacks/Mind Thrust|mind thrust]]* *,* *[[Psionic Blast (PB)|psionic blast]]* *,* and [[Psychic Crush (PsC)|psychic crush]] . Psionicists have access to all five forms (depending on their levels), whereas wild talents can never have more than three of the five. The psionic attacks are described below.
