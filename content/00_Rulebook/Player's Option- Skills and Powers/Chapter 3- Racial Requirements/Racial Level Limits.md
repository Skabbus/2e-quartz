Racial Ability Adjustments Once the character’s ability scores have been determined, the race chosen, and the [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 3- Racial Requirements/Table 14- Racial Requirements|Racial Requirements table]] consulted, modify theability scores based on the [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 3- Racial Requirements/Table 15- Racial Adjustments|Racial Adjustments table]]—and determine the subability scores as per the rules in [[Subability Checks|chapter two]].

Racial Level Limits The Racial Level Limits chart lists the maximum experience levels attainable by the various races. For more on classes, see [[Table 19- Warrior Experience Levels|chapter four]].

Exceeding These LimitsThe *[Dungeon Master Guide](..\DMG\DD00221.htm)* included an optional rule where demihuman characters could exceed level limits if they possessed high prime-requisite ability scores. This rule is official in the *Player’s Option* system. See the [[Table 17- Prime Requisite Bonuses|chart below]] for bonus levels granted to those characters with high scores in their class prime requisites.
