**Purchasing Weapon Proficiencies Out of Class**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Character** | **Class Weapon** | **Character Point** |
| **Class** | **is From** | **Cost** |
| Rogue | Warrior | 4 |
| Priest | Warrior | 4 |
| Wizard | Rogue | 5 |
| Wizard | Warrior | 6 |

Warriors can purchase a proficiency in any single weapon at a cost of 2 character points.
