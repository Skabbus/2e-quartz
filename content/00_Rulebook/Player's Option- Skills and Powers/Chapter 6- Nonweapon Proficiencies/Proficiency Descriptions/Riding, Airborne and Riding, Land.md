**Riding, Airborne and Riding, Land**

:** The riding proficiencies are well-detailed in the *[Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01588.htm)*. Characters using the Skills and Powers rules can add +2 to their proficiency score in either category of riding if they possess the trait of [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 6- Nonweapon Proficiencies/Trait Descriptions/Animal Empathy|animal empathy]], and +1 if they have the additional proficiency in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 6- Nonweapon Proficiencies/Proficiency Descriptions/Animal Training|animal training]]. These modifiers are cumulative.
