**Compulsive Honesty**

:** The character with this disadvantage cannot tell a lie and cannot behave in a deceitful fashion. He tends to be blunt rather than tactful, even if this means insulting someone who he and his companions are trying to impress.

The character could participate in a deception—but only if it is a matter of life and death. Even then, the character must make a Wisdom/Willpower check every time he is called upon to speak a falsehood or to act out a role in front of witnesses. Failure of the role means that the character shrugs and comes clean—*”You’re right, my lord. I’m not here in the marketplace to shop for rugs, but to steal the plans for the castle’s defenses.”*[[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Table of Contents#d57de441|Table of Contents]]
