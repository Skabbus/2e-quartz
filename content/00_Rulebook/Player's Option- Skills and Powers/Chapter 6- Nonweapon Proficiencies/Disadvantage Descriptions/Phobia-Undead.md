**Phobia—Undead:** As with the other specific creature phobias, this fear requires a Wisdom/Willpower check at the beginning of an encounter. Further, the character must pass a check before he can enter a location where he reasonably expects undead to be. This latter check can be repeated at 2–12 turn intervals, if necessary.
