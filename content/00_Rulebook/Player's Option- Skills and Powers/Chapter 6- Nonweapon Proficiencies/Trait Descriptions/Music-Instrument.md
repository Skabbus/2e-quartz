**Music/Instrument:** The character with this trait has the manual dexterity and musical sensibility to skillfully operate an instrument such as a harp, flute, lyre, drum, etc. While the trait does not convey the knowledge of how to play any instrument葉hat must come from a proficiency葉his inherent talent assures that the character may quite possibly rise to a significant level of fame for his musical performances. The character can more readily learn to play musical instruments as note in the musical instrument proficiency description.
