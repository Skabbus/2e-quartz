Specialty Priests

Specialty priests are encountered in campaigns where DMs develop or import a mythos or a particular pantheon of deities. The many possible types of specialty priests go beyond the scope of this book. However, players and DMs interested in designing these priests should consult the *[Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01481.htm)*, the *Complete Priests Handbook*, and the *Legends & Lore* book.
