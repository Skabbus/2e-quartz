Rogues

Rogues are scoundrels, living by their wits day to day—often at the expense of others. Not all rogues are outright criminals, but many of them possess a shady past they’d rather not have made public. Rogues have several special abilities, the success of which is determined by using percentile dice. Refer to the rogue section of the *[Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01498.htm)* for more information. There are two classes of rogues—[[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 4- Character Classes/Rogues/Thief|thieves]] and [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 4- Character Classes/Rogues/Bard|bards]] . Both use the following table to dictate their level advancements. All rogues roll 1d6 for their hit points from 1st through 10th level. After that, rogues add 2 hit points per level.
