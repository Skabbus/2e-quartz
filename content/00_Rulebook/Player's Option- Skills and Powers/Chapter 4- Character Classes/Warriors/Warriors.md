Warriors

Warriors make their way in the world by the strength of their muscles and the swiftness of their swords. Warrior classes include the [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 4- Character Classes/Warriors/Fighter|fighter]] , [[Table 20- Paladin Spell Progression|paladin]] , and [[Table 22- Ranger Abilities By level|ranger]] . Using the Player’s Option system, all members of the warrior group:· Have the ability to select any weapon and wear any type of armor available in the campaign.· Roll d10s to determine their hit points from 1st to 9th level. Thereafter, they gain three hit points per level.· Are prevented from using certain magical items.· Can become proficient in “shield,” which grants them an Armor Class bonus. See the [[Dexterity, Aim, Balance#5f05efeb|proficiencies section]] for more details.· At higher levels make additional melee attacks in a combat round. The chart below details this progression.

Use the [[Table 19- Warrior Experience Levels|Table 19]] to determine the rate at which they advance in levels.
