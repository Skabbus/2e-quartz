**Recording Kits on the Character Sheet**

To record your priest kit on your character record sheet, do the following:(1) When you write down the character's priest-class, also add the name of his priest kit there. If the character were a priest of the Norse god of thunder and also a peasant priest, you'd write "Priest of Thor/Peasant Priest."(2) Where you write down the character's nonweapon proficiencies, add the ones you got free from the priest kit, and put an asterisk beside them to indicate that they are free proficiencies provided by the kit.(3) Wherever you have space for notes, mark down the character's special benefits and hindrances, and any other facts you want to remember.
