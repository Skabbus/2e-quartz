> | 10 yards/level | 10-foot-cube/level |
> | **Casting Time** | **Duration** |
> | 6 | 1 day/level |
> | **Save** | **Damage** |
> | None | — |
> | **Components** | **Material** |
> | S V M | The priest's holy symbol and a pinch of volcanic ash |
> 
> *Land of stability* protects the area of effect and all creatures and objects within it from the following natural disasters:•Earthquakes--vibrations do not affect the warded area and fissures will not open beneath the warded area;•Floods--the warded area remains dry, even if submerged;•Windstorms--the warded area suffers no damage from strong winds and objects cannot be blown into the warded area;•Lava and ash eruptions--lava and ash flow around the warded area; and•Avalanches--stones and snow will not fall on the warded area. *Land of stability* offers no protection against magically-generated disasters or spells that duplicate natural disasters. Disasters in progress in the area when the spell is cast are not affected.
> 
> This spell affects a cubic area whose sides equal the caster's level times 10 feet; thus, a 15th-level caster could affect a 150' x 150' x 150' cube.
> 
> The material components are the priest's holy symbol and a pinch of volcanic ash.
