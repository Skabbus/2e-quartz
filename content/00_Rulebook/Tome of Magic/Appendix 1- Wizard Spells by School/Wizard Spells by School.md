**Appendix 1: Wizard Spells by School**Boldfaced** spells are described in the *Tome of Magic* rule book. The remainder are found in the AD&D® 2nd Edition *Player's Handbook*.*Italicized* spells are reversible. The reverse name follows the slash.**

An asterisk (\*) indicates a Wild Magic spell.
