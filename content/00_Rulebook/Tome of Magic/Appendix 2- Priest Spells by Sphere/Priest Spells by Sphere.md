**Appendix 2: Priest Spells by Sphere**Boldfaced** spells are described in the *Tome of Magic* rule book. The remaining spellsare found in the 2nd Edition *Player's Handbook*.*Italicized* spells are reversible. The reverse name follows the slash.**

An asterisk (\*) indicates a cooperative magic spell.
