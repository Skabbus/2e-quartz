***Sword of Sharpness***

The magical properties of *sharpness* override the normal critical hit procedure. It is still possible to achieve a normal critical hit by rolling an 18 or 19, even if it doesn’t quite activate the *sharpness* properties. If a limb is severed, consider the injury to be a critical one of the appropriate sort—[[Specific Injuries#8558030f|Chapter Six]] details the unpleasant effects of losing limbs.
