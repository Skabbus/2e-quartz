***Darkness, 15' Radius***Creatures outside the area of darkness cannot trace a line of fire through the darkness to a target on the other side. Creatures moving in total darkness are reduced to 1/3 their normal move unless they wish to risk stumbling[[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Table of Contents#d57de195|Table of Contents]]
