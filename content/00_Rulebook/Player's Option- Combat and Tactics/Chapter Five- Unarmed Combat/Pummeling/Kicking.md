**Kicking**

Characters attacking with a height advantage (or attacking a prone, kneeling, or sitting opponent) can pummel by kicking instead of punching. Humanoid characters tend to have stronger legs than arms, and inflict extra damage:· **Bare feet:** 1d3 points of damage;· **Shoes, boots:** 1d4 points of damage;· **Heavy boots:** 1d6 points of damage;· **Iron-shod or hobnailed boots:** 1d8 points of damage.
