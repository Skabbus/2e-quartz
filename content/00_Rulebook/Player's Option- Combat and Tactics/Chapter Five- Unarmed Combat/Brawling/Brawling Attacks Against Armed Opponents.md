Brawling Attacks AgainstArmed OpponentsAny brawling attack provokes an attack of opportunity from any and all opponents who are armed and threatening the assailant. The defender gets a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls. If an attack of opportunity slays the attacker, there is no brawling attack. If an attack of opportunity creates a knockdown chance, the brawling attack automatically fails and is wasted, even if the attacker’s saving throw succeeds and he is not knocked down—the defender’s weapon keeps the attacker at bay. If an attack of opportunity results in a critical hit, the character who scored the hit can choose to roll for the critical hit (see [[Bludgeoning vs. Humanoids, critical hit table#bcdf214c|Chapter Six]]) or force the brawling attack to automatically fail.
