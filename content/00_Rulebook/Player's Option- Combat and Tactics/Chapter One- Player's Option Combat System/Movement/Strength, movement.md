**Strength**Add the character’s hit probability adjustment to her base movement rate. If you are also playing with the [Skills & Powers](..\SP\DD02868.htm#c270fabb)rulebook, add the hit probability adjustment based on the character’s Strength/Stamina (not Strength/Muscle) instead.
