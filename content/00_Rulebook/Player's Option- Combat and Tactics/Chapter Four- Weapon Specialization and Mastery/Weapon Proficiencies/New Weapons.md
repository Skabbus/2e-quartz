**New Weapons**

A number of new weapons are introduced in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Option- Combat and Tactics/Chapter Seven- Weapons and Armor/Master Weapon List/Weapon Descriptions|Chapter Seven]]: Weapons and Armor. Many of these are similar to weapons that were restricted in the *Player’s Handbook*. The Weapon Table in [[Master Weapons Table|Chapter Seven]] lists any classes that cannot use a particular weapon under normal circumstances.
