Sieges

Sieges are ponderous affairs that involve a lot of waiting and general inactivity. The attacker’s main goal is to batter down the defending walls so they can launch an escalade or to drive the defender out through starvation or thirst. The following system allows you to simulate the effects of an extended siege, handling months of operations with a single die roll.
