> By means of this spell, the wizard creates the illusion of a door. The illusion also permits the wizard to appear to step through this “door” and disappear. In reality, he has darted aside and can flee, totally invisible, for the spell duration. Creatures viewing this are deluded into seeing or entering an empty 10-foot x 10-foot room if they open the “door.” A *true seeing* spell, a *gem of seeing,* or similar magical means can discover the wizard. Certain high Hit Dice monsters might also notice the wizard (see the [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Second-Level Spells/Invisibility|invisibility]] spell), but only if making an active attempt to do so.
