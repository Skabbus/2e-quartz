Death From Poison

Poison complicates this situation, somewhat. A character who dies as a result of poisoning may still have active venom in his system.

Poisons remain effective for 2d6 hours after the death of the victim. If the character is raised during this time, some method must be found to neutralize the poison before the character is restored to life. If this is not done, then after the character rolls the resurrection survival check as given in “[[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Chapter 9- Combat/Character Death/Raising the Dead|Raising the Dead]]” later in this chapter (and assuming the roll is successful), he must immediately roll a successful saving throw vs. poison or suffer all the effects of the poison in his body, as per the normal rules. This may only injure some characters, but it may kill other characters seconds after being raised![[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Table of Contents#e6fca08a|Table of Contents]]
