**Where's the Specific Info?**

**The preceding text has covered general guidelines as to how and why characters receive experience points. Since the DM actually determines how many XP each character actually receives, the detailed rules for awarding experience are given in the *[Dungeon Master Guide](..\DMG\DD00372.htm)**.*[[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Table of Contents#e6fca08a|Table of Contents]]
