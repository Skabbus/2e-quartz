## Pathfinder

- Ninja (Character Kit)

**Qualifications:** None beyond normal ninja requirements. This kit is available to humans, half-elves, and halflings.

**Description and Role:** Pathfinder ninja possesses special wilderness experience and abilities.

**Secondary Skills:** Forester, Hunter, Trapper/Furrier (choose one).

**Weapon Proficiencies:** *Required:* Half-bow (hankyu), ninja-to.

**Nonweapon Proficiencies:**

* *Bonus:* [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Chapter 5- Proficiencies (Optional)/Nonweapon Proficiencies II/Nonweapon Proficiency Descriptions/Tracking|Tracking]].
* *Required:* [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Chapter 5- Proficiencies (Optional)/Nonweapon Proficiencies II/Nonweapon Proficiency Descriptions/Hunting|Hunting]].

**Special Benefits:** The Pathfinder receives extra bonuses to Tracking proficiency: +1 at 5th level, another +1 at 9th level, and another +1 at 13th level.

**Special Hindrances:** This ninja gathers no followers.
