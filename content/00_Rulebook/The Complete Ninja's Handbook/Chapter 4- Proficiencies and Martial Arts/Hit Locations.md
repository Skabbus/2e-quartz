## Hit Locations

Players who like to visualize where their characters' blows are landing can use a variant of the Martial Arts Results Table (presented earlier).

Each time the character attacks with martial arts, he makes a die roll and consults Table 23 to determine the location of the blow. The principal method of the martial art determines the type of roll.
