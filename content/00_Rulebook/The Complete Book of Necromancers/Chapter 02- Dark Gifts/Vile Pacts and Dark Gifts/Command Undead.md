**Command Undead**

This power enables the necromancer to command undead like an evil priest with half as many levels of experience. Thus an 18th-level necromancer controls undead as a 9th-level priest. Certain necromancer kits and some necromantic priesthoods already have authority over undead (the DM should choose another salient ability for such characters).
