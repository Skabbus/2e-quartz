**Wizard Kit Creation Sheet**

At the end of this book is a blank Kit Creation Sheet. Copies of this sheet may be made to help you design new kits. The DM or players can design new kits appropriate to the character types they would like to play. However, if a player designs his own kit, he should check all details of the kit with his DM. The DM may make changes, or he may disallow the kit altogether[[00_Rulebook/The Complete Wizard's Handbook/Table of Contents|Table of Contents]]
