## Boating

(Proficiency)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Boating |
| Crossover Group | General |
| Relevant Ability | Wisdom |
| Check Modifier | +1 |
| # of Slots | 1 Slot |

This proficiency lets the character pilot small boats, including canoes, rafts, and kayaks. A successful proficiency check is necessary to pilot them at maximum speed or to execute a difficult maneuver, such as steering around rocks in a rapid river or spearing a fish without capsizing.

**Barbarians:** A barbarian may not take this proficiency unless water vessels are common in his homeland.

**Crossover Group:** General.
