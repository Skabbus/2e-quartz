## Multi-Class Barbarians

The primary multi-class open to a barbarian is the dwarf fighter/cleric demi-barbarian, using any combination of kits available to dwarf demi-barbarians. The dwarf advances in both classes simultaneously, up to 14th level (fighter) and 12th level (cleric). All rules for [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Chapter 3- Player Character Classes/Class Descriptions/Multi-Class and Dual-Class Characters/Multi-Class and Dual-Class Characters|multi-class characters from Chapter]] 3 of the *Player's Handbook* also apply. The DM may permit other multi-class barbarian races.
