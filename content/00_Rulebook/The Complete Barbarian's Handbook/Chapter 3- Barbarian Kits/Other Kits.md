## Other Kits

Other kits dealing with barbarians have appeared previously — there have even been a few kits for wizards from barbaric tribes! Previous treatments of the barbarian theme include:

* *The Complete Fighter:* [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Barbarian|Barbarian]], [[Beast-Riders|Beast Rider]], [[Berserker|Berserker]], [[00_Rulebook/The Complete Fighter's Handbook/Warrior Kits/The Warrior Kits/Savage|Savage]], [[Wilderness Warrior|Wilderness Warrior]].
* *The Complete Priest:* [[Barbarian-Berserker Priest|Barbarian/Berserker Priest]], [[Savage Priest|Savage Priest]].
* *The Complete Wizard:* [[Anagakok|Anagakok]], [[Savage Wizard|Savage Wizard]].
* *The Complete Bard:* [[Skald|Skald]].
* *The Complete Ranger:* [[Feralan|Feralan]].

If any of these are considered for use, the DM should decide how and if they fit into the campaign setting.
