## Dual-Classed Barbarians

Human barbarians can become dual-classed, but only as barbarian fighter/clerics. They have access to any compatible character kit allowed in the campaign.

To change from a barbarian fighter to a shaman, the character must have a minimum score of 15 in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Appendix 8- Compiled Character Generation Tables/Table 1- Strength|Strength]], and a minimum score of 17 in [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Appendix 8- Compiled Character Generation Tables/Table 2- Dexterity|Dexterity]], [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Appendix 8- Compiled Character Generation Tables/Table 3- Constitution|Constitution]], and [[00_Rulebook/Player's Handbook/Appendix 8- Compiled Character Generation Tables/Table 5- Wisdom|Wisdom]].

To change from a shaman to a barbarian fighter, the character must have a minimum score of 15 in Wisdom, and a minimum score of 17 in Dexterity, Constitution, and Strength.
