**Alertness**

A character with this proficiency is exceptionally attuned to his surroundings, able to detect disturbances and notice discrepancies. A successful proficiency check reduces his chance of being surprised by 1. (This replaces the description of this proficiency in *The Complete Thief's Handbook*.)

**Crossover Groups:** General.
