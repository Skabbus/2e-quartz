> This spell evaporates moisture from the bodies of every living creature within the area of effect, inflicting 1d8 points of damage per level of the caster. Affected creatures are allowed a saving throw, with success indicating half damage. This spell is especially devastating to water elementals and plant creatures, who receive a penalty of -2 to their saving throws.
