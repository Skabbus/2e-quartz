Ability Score Requirements

Just like demihumans, characters must qualify for these optional races by meeting certain ability score criteria. The [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 3- Racial Requirements/Racial Abilitiy Score Adjustments|ability score adjustments]] noted are applied after a character has met the required scores. Some of these adjustments can elevate a humanoid or monstrous character to ability scores of 19 or 20, or reduce a characteristic to 2.
