Psionics in a Round

A psionicist can do several actions during a single round. He can:· Make as many psionic attacks as his experience level indicates.· Use one psionic defense.· Activate one psionic power against an open mind.· Maintain as many previously successfully activated powers as he wishes, provided he can afford to continue paying the PSP costs. Further, if a psionic attack succeeds and opens a closed mind, a psionic power can be used against the mind that same round.
