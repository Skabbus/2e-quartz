Mind Blank (MBk)

This defense hides the mind from psionic attack, forming a vast, featureless area that makes it harder to target the closed mind. Mind blank protects best against id insinuation, while mind thrust easily slices through the defensive fog. It costs 3 PSPs per round to use this defense.
