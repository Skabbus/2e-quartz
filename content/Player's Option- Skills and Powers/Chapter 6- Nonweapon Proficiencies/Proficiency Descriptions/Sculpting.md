**Sculpting**

:** The character with this proficiency can render realistic objects out of stone and clay. A high level of sculpting proficiency, coupled with the [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 6- Nonweapon Proficiencies/Trait Descriptions/Artistic Ability|artistic]] talent trait, means the character can create statues, statuettes, busts, and other objects of rare and valuable beauty.
