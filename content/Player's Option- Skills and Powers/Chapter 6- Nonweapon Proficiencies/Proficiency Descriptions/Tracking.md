**Tracking**

:** The detailed tracking procedure described in the *[Player’s Handbook](..\PHB\DD01601.htm)* is modified as follows for the *Skills and Powers* rules:

No characters suffer the integral –6 penalty to their ability scores; this difference is reflected in the proficiency rating itself.

Rangers gain a +5 bonus to their tracking rating.

Characters with the [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 6- Nonweapon Proficiencies/Trait Descriptions/Animal Empathy|animal empathy]] trait gain +2 to their proficiency score when tracking non-domesticated animals.

Characters with the [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 6- Nonweapon Proficiencies/Proficiency Descriptions/Animal Lore|animal lore]] proficiency gain +2 to their proficiency rating when tracking animals—either wild or domesticated.
