**Endurance**

:** A character with this proficiency can perform continual strenuous physical activity for twice as long as a normal character before becoming exhausted. If the character is ever required to make a Strength/Stamina check or a Constitution/Fitness check, the character can add his endurance score to his success number. If the fatigue rules from the *Player’s Option: Combat & Tactics* book are in play, the endurance proficiency is treated differently (see *[Combat & Tactics](..\CT\DD02440.htm)*, Chapter One).
