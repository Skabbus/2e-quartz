**Internal Compass**

:** Characters with this trait have a general idea of where they are, and in which direction they are facing when out of doors. In the wilderness, this means that their chance of becoming lost is reduced by 5%. When using the navigation proficiency, characters with this trait receive a +1 bonus to their proficiency score.
