**Keen Taste Sense**

:** This trait allows characters to detect foreign substances and unnatural alterations in anything they taste. Characters can make Wisdom/Intuition checks with a +3 modifier. Success means they realize some sort of alteration has been done to the food or drink. Halflings can purchase this trait for 1 less character point than the listed amount.
