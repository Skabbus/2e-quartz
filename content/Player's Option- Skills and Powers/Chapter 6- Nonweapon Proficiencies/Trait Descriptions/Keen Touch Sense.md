**Keen Touch Sense**

:** A character with this trait has unusually sensitive tactile senses, able to feel the difference between a silver and gold piece, for example. If the character with this trait is a thief, this inherent advantage gives a +5% bonus to [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 4- Character Classes/Rogues/Thief#2c856baa|pick pockets]] and [[Player's Option- Skills and Powers/Chapter 4- Character Classes/Rogues/Thief#57e36f71|open locks]] attempts. Gnomes can purchase this trait for 1 less character point than the listed amount.
