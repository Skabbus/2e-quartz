**Climate Sense**

:** This trait provides a character with an innate sense of impending (within one hour) changes in the weather. The DM might require a Wisdom/Intuition check before the character can make the determination. Other circumstances may be obvious enough that the character will know with certainty that the temperature will fall drastically, or that a tremendous rainstorm is about to begin.
