**Chapter 13:**

**Vision and Light** Before a character can do anything in the dungeon or the wilderness, he has to be able to see what he is doing. If a character can't see a target, his chances of hitting it are very small. If he can't see, he can't read a scroll or a large “Keep Out” sign on the wall. In the AD&D game, characters can see set distances and often by fantastic means that defy logic.
