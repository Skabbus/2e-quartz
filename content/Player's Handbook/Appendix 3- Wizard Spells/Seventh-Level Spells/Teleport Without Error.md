> This spell is similar to the *teleport* spell. The caster is able to transport himself, along with the material weight noted for a *teleport* spell, to any known location in his home plane with no chance for error. The spell also enables the caster to travel to other planes of existence, but any such plane is, at best, “studied carefully.” This assumes that the caster has, in fact, actually been to the plane and carefully perused an area for an eventual *teleportation without error* spell. The table for the *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Fifth-Level Spells/Teleport|teleport]]* spell is used, with the caster's knowledge of the area to which transportation is desired used to determine the chance of error. (For an exception, see the 9th-level wizard spell *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Ninth-Level Spells/Succor|succor]]*.) The caster can do nothing else in the round that he appears from a teleport.
