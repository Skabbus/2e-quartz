> The *erase* spell removes writings of either magical or mundane nature from a scroll or from one to two pages of paper, parchment, or similar surfaces. It removes *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Third-Level Spells/Explosive Runes|explosive runes]]*,*[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 4- Priest Spells/Third-Level Spells/Glyph of Warding|glyphs of warding]],* *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Third-Level Spells/Sepia Snake Sigil|sepia snake sigils]]*, and *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/First-Level Spells/Wizard Mark|wizard marks]]*, but it does not remove *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Third-Level Spells/Illusionary Script|illusory script]]* or [[Player's Handbook/Appendix 3- Wizard Spells/Eighth-Level Spells/Symbol|symbols]] (see those spells). Nonmagical writings are automatically erased if the caster is touching them; otherwise, the chance for success is 90%. Magical writings must be touched, and are only 30% likely to be erased, plus 5% per caster level, to a maximum of 90% (for example, 35% for a 1st-level caster, 40% for a 2nd-level caster, etc.).
