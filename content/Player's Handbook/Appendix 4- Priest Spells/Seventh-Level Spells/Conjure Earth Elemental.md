> A caster who performs a *conjure earth elemental* spell summons an earth elemental to do his bidding. The elemental is 60% likely to have 12 Hit Dice, 35% likely to have 16 Hit Dice, and 5% likely have 21 to 24 Hit Dice (20 + 1d4). Further, the caster needs but to command it, and it does as desired. The elemental regards the caster as a friend to be obeyed. The elemental remains until destroyed, dispelled, sent away by dismissal or a *holy word* spell (see the *[[Player's Handbook/Appendix 4- Priest Spells/Sixth-Level Spells/Conjure Fire Elemental|conjure fire elemental]]* spell), or the spell duration expires.
