**Special Defenses**

So far, the bulk of this chapter has dealt with ways to attack. Now, it's time to turn to defense. There are several ways to avoid taking damage. Two of the most common are the *[[Player's Handbook/Chapter 9- Combat/Definitions/Saving Throws|saving throw]]* and [[Player's Handbook/Chapter 9- Combat/Magic Resistance/Magic Resistance|magic resistance]]*.* Somewhat less common, because its use is limited to clerics and paladins, is the ability to *turn undead.*[[Player's Handbook/Table of Contents#e6fca08a|Table of Contents]]
