**Table 63:**

**Light Sources**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | **Radius** | **Burning time** |
| Beacon lantern | 240 ft.\* | 30 hrs./pint |
| Bonfire | 50 ft. | ½ hr./armload |
| Bullseye lantern | 60 ft.\* | 2 hrs./pint |
| Campfire | 35 ft. | 1 hr./armload |
| Candle | 5 ft. | 10 min./inch |
| *Continual light* | 60 ft. | Indefinite |
| Hooded lantern | 30 ft. | 2 hrs./pint |
| *Light* spell | 20 ft. | Variable |
| Torch | 15 ft. | 30 min. |
| Weapon\*\* | 5 ft. | As desired |

\* Light from these is not cast in a radius, but rather in a cone-shaped beam. At its far end, the cone of light from a beacon lantern is 90 feet wide. A bullseye lantern has a beam 20 feet wide at its far end.\*\* Magical weapons shed light if your DM allows this optional rule.
