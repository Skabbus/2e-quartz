**Swimming**

A character with swimming proficiency knows how to swim and can move according to the rules given in the Swimming section ([[Player's Handbook/Chapter 14- Time and Movement/Time and Movement|Chapter 14]]: Time and Movement). Those without this proficiency cannot swim. They can hold their breath and float, but they cannot move themselves about in the water.
