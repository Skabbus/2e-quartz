**Reading/Writing** 

The character can read and write a modern language he can speak, provided there is someone available to teach the character (another PC, a hireling, or an NPC). This proficiency does not enable the character to learn ancient languages (see [[Player's Handbook/Chapter 5- Proficiencies (Optional)/Nonweapon Proficiencies II/Nonweapon Proficiency Descriptions/Languages, Ancient|Languages, Ancient]]).
