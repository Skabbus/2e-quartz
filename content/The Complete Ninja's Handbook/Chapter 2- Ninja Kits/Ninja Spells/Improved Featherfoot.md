## Improved Featherfoot

(Wizard Spell)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Improved Featherfoot (Alteration) |
| Class | Wizard |
| Spell Level | 4 |
| Range | Touch |
| Components | S |
| Duration | 1 turn/level |
| Casting Time | 1 round |
| Area of Effect | One creature |
| Saving Throw | None |

This spell improves on the 2nd-level *[featherfoot](Featherfoot_(Wizard_Spell).htm)* spell by additionally allowing the ninja to walk across still or gently moving water surfaces (the water of a pond or a slow river, for example).

Such travel leaves the bottoms of the ninja's feet wet, so she must take care to leave no trail after leaving the water.
