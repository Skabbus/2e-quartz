## Nonweapon Proficiencies

As you'll recall from [[The Complete Ninja's Handbook/Chapter 1- The Ninja Class/The Ninja Class/The Ninja Class#Nonweapon_Proficiencies|Chapter 1]], ninja purchase nonweapon proficiencies from the General, Rogue, and Warrior groups without penalty, and from the Wizard and Priest groups with the standard penalties. Ninja with the [Spirit Warrior](Spirit_Warrior_(Character_Kit).htm) kit can take proficiencies from the Wizard group without penalty.
