## Killers

Another type of character derived from the ninja character class is the killer. This variant NPC is a murderer who needs ninja abilities to get to his target.

Any culture can produce killer organizations. Killers with the Eliminator kit are best suited to the same types of cultures that produce spies, while those with the Punisher and Ravager kits most often appear in cultures considered foreign and exotic.

## Restrictions

Player characters cannot belong to the killer (ninja) class. These rules are included for the DM who wants to create extremely dangerous opponents for his PCs.

## Killer Kits

There are three kits available for killers: [Eliminator](Eliminator_(Character_Kit).htm), [Punisher](Punisher_(Character_Kit).htm), and [Ravager](Ravager_(Character_Kit).htm).
