## Shinobi Ranger

(Character Kit)

**Qualifications:** This kit is available to human and half-elf rangers only.

**Description and Role:** The Shinobi Ranger is the clan wilderness specialist. However, this character's primary concern is neither protecting the wilderness nor making her livelihood there. The Shinobi Ranger's skills are used to scout enemies and lead clan members through the wilderness so they can perform missions successfully.

**Secondary Skills:** Trapper/Furrier.

**Weapon Proficiencies:** *Required:* Hankyu (half-bow).

**Nonweapon Proficiencies:** *Required:* [[Player's Handbook/Chapter 5- Proficiencies (Optional)/Nonweapon Proficiencies II/Nonweapon Proficiency Descriptions/Running|Running]].

**Special Benefits:** Like the Shinobi Fighter, the Shinobi Ranger can learn thieving skills. Her skills are move silently, hide in shadows, and detect noise, with the same base scores and progression as a Shinobi Fighter.

At 1st level he can move silently, hide in shadows, and detect noise with base scores of 10% each (modified by all racial bonuses and penalties). With each subsequent experience level, the Shinobi gets 10 discretionary points to divide among the three thieving skills.

#### Shinobi Ranger Base Scores

| Skill | Base Score |
| --- | --- |
| Move Silently | 10% |
| Hide in Shadows | 10% |
| Detect Noise | 10% |

*Special Hindrances: **None.
