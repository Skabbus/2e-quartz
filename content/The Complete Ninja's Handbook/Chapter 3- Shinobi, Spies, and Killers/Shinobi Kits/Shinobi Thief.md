## Shinobi Thief

(Character Kit)

**Qualifications:** This kit is available to humans, dwarves, half-elves, and halfling thieves.

**Description and Role:** In a ninja clan, the character who wants to have a good balance between fighting and thief skills becomes a ninja. The Shinobi Thief is a specialist in thieving skills, as dedicated to them as a graybeard scholar is to reading musty scrolls by candlelight. The Shinobi Thief's fighting skills are not on a par with that of the ninja or the ordinary thief, but his thief skills are superior.

The Shinobi Thief often works on missions side by side with true ninja and is a welcome brother-in-arms. In missions requiring several party members, the Shinobi Thief is the intrusion specialist, the one relied on for finding and removing traps and other security devices.

**Secondary Skills:** None required.

**Weapon Proficiencies:**

* *Required:* Ninja-to.

**Nonweapon Proficiencies:** No bonuses, requirements, or prohibitions.

**Special Benefits:** The Shinobi Thief starts out with thieving skills superior to those of a regular thief.

Like a regular thief, the Shinobi Thief receives 60 discretionary points at 1st level (no more than 30 points may be added to any one skill) and receives 30 points at each additional experience level. (No more than 15 points may be added to any one skill.)

**Special Hindrances:** The Shinobi Thief can wear only padded or leather armor, and can use only the same weapons as wizards (dagger, knife, staff, dart, sling). Like a wizard, the Shinobi Thief uses a four-sided die to roll for hit points.
