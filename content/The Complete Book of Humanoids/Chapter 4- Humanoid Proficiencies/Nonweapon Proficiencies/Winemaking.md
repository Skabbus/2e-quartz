## Winemaking

(Proficiency)

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Winemaking |
| Group(s) | General |
| Ability | Inteligence |
| Modifier | 0 |
| Slots | 1 |

This proficiency allows characters to create wine from the fermented juice of grapes or other plants and fruits well enough to make a living at it. The character will always succeed to some extent; proficiency checks are only required when attempting to prepare a truly magnificent wine as a special gift or for a special celebration.
